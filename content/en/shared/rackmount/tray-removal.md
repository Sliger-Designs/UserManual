Removing the tray is optional, but can make component installation easier.

### 1. Remove the tray screws

Remove the four {{ hw("E") }} securing the tray's rear panel to the chassis, two on each side.

### 2. Slide the tray back

Move the tray toward the rear until all six round metal tabs disengage from the tray.

### 3. Lift the tray out

Lift only after the tray has cleared the tabs. Do not force it upward while it remains engaged.
