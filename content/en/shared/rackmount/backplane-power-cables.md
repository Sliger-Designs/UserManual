The {{ hw('L') }} power the backplane's two 4-pin inputs. Use one of the two options described in {{ ref('backplane-connections', 'Connect the backplane') }}.

- **EPS/CPU 8-pin to dual 4-pin:** one Y-cable with a 300 mm and a 500 mm branch.
- **PCIe 6-pin to 4-pin:** two adapters, 300 mm each. Four of the six positions are wired.

The face views look into the connector openings with the latch on the right. Yellow positions carry +12 V and grey positions are ground. Lengths are ±10 mm.
