### 1. Install the standoffs

Match standoff positions to the mounting holes on your motherboard. Install the {{ hw("C") }} into the corresponding threaded locations on the tray.

Do not leave an extra standoff beneath a part of the board without a mounting hole.

### 2. Install the I/O shield

If your motherboard has a separate I/O shield, orient it to match the motherboard ports and press it into the rectangular opening in the tray's rear panel.

<p class="info-note" role="note">Skip this step if the I/O shield is integrated into the motherboard.</p>
