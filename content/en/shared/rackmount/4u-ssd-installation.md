### 1. Position the SSD

The rear of the motherboard tray has two pairs of 2.5-inch mounting holes. Install either two SSDs up to 7 mm thick, or one SSD up to 15 mm thick. Align the drive with the mounting holes shown. Keep its connectors accessible and check clearance to the PSU and motherboard.

### 2. Secure the drives

Secure each drive with two {{ hw('B') }} through the tray. Two 7 mm drives use four screws in total. Tighten by hand without forcing the threads.

For M.2 storage, follow the motherboard's installation instructions.
