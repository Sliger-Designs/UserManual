### 1. Place the cage

Lower the populated drive cage onto the tray and secure it with the two {{ hw('A') }}.

### 2. Choose the cage position

The cage fits in either of two positions on the tray, to make room for the cooling and motherboard arrangement. Route radiator tubes on the side opposite the power switch to leave room for the cage.

### 3. Secure the side screw

Install the {{ hw('E') }} through the side of the chassis into the cage at the chosen position. Connect the drive cables without obstructing fans or the motherboard tray.
