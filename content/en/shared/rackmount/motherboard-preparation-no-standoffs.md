This chassis uses no motherboard standoffs: the board mounts directly to the threaded mounting points on the tray.

### 1. Install the I/O shield

If your motherboard has a separate I/O shield, orient it to match the motherboard ports and press it into the rectangular opening in the tray's rear panel.

<p class="info-note" role="note">Skip this step if the I/O shield is integrated into the motherboard.</p>
