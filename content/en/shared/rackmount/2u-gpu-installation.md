### 1. Prepare the slot

Select the PCIe slot specified by your motherboard manual. Remove the corresponding chassis slot covers and open the motherboard slot latch, if present.

### 2. Seat the card

Fit a low-profile (half-height) bracket to the card. Align the card with the motherboard slot and rear bracket opening, then apply gentle, even pressure until it is fully seated.

### 3. Secure the bracket

Fasten the card's mounting bracket to the chassis with {{ hw("A") }}. Fit {{ hw("G") }} over unused openings and secure them with {{ hw("A") }}.

<p class="info-note" role="note">Card height, including power connectors, must remain within <strong>{{ facts.gpu_height_max_mm }} mm</strong>. Cards may be listed as 2U height, low-profile, or MD1/MD2/MD3 form factor. Check the cooling-dependent length limits before installation.</p>
