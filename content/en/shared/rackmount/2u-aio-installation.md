The Dynatron L34 2U AIO mounts on the subfront in place of the front fans, with its four 80 mm fans between the subfront and the radiator.

### 1. Remove the fan guards

Remove all the screws holding the fans and fan guards to the radiator, leaving the fans on the radiator. Set the fan guards aside; they are not used in the chassis. Keep the screws.

### 2. Mount the radiator

Hold the radiator and fans against the inside of the subfront, fans toward the front. Secure them through the subfront with half of the radiator screws, passing each screw through a fan into the radiator. Route the tubing to the CPU block clear of the PCIe slots.

### Check component clearance
