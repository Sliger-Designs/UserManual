### 1. Lower the motherboard

Place the motherboard onto the tray's mounting points. Align its rear ports with the I/O opening and its mounting holes with the threaded mounting points.

### 2. Secure the board

Install {{ hw("A") }} through the board into the mounting points. Tighten by hand to avoid damaging the board.

Follow your motherboard's instructions for CPU, memory, M.2 drives, and cable connections.
