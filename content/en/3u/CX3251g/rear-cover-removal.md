The rear cover closes the three horizontal PCIe slots above the riser, from outside the rear panel. Remove it before installing the GPU.

### 1. Remove the cover screws

From the back of the chassis, remove the two {{ hw('A') }} securing the rear cover.

### 2. Slide the cover toward the PSU

Slide the cover about 10–15 mm toward the PSU to free it from the rear panel.

### 3. Remove the cover

Pull the cover straight back, away from the chassis, and set it aside with its screws for reassembly.
