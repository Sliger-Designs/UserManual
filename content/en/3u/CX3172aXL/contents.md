This guide covers chassis access, component installation, and reassembly.

Choose your cooling configuration and check component clearances before starting assembly. Supported components may compete for the same space.

**Before installation:** Follow the installation instructions supplied with your motherboard, power supply, graphics card, and cooler.

**Maintenance:** Before opening a chassis that already has components installed, switch the system off and unplug it.

**Fasteners:** Tighten chassis fasteners by hand. Do not force a screw that does not thread correctly.
