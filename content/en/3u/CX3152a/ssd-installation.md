The motherboard tray has two 2.5-inch mounting locations: one flat under the power supply and one upright beside the motherboard.

### 1. Mount the SSD under the PSU

Before installing the PSU, place the SSD flat on the tray, align its mounting holes and secure it with {{ hw('B') }} through the tray.

### 2. Mount the SSD beside the motherboard

Position the second SSD upright with its connectors facing upward and secure it with {{ hw('B') }} through the tray wall.

<p class="info-note" role="note">For M.2 storage, follow the motherboard's installation instructions.</p>
