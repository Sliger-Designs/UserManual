The 5.25-inch bay filler blank holds four 2.5-inch SSDs, two 2.5-inch SSDs up to 15 mm thick, or one 3.5-inch HDD.

### 1. Mount four 2.5-inch SSDs

Place the SSDs in the blank, align their side mounting holes with the holes in the blank, and secure each drive with {{ hw('B') }}.

### 2. Or mount two 15 mm SSDs

Align the SSDs' bottom mounting holes with the underside of the blank and secure each drive with {{ hw('B') }}.

### 3. Or mount a 3.5-inch HDD

Align the HDD's bottom mounting holes with the underside of the blank and install four {{ hw('E') }} through the blank into the drive.

<p class="info-note" role="note">Mount drives on the blank before fitting the blank in the bay bracket, and route their cables clear of the front cooling assembly.</p>
