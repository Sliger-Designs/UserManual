### 1. Position the fans

Place two 120 mm fans between the subfront and the 240 mm radiator. Remove any fan screws occupying the radiator mounting positions.

### 2. Align and secure the assembly

Align all eight mounting points in the panel, fans, and radiator. Use the cooler manufacturer's specified radiator screws through the panel and fan frames into the radiator. Incorrect screw length can damage the radiator.

### 3. Check the 5.25-inch device screws

The radiator end can touch a hex-head screw retaining a device in the bay, as shown. Use a countersunk screw there, {{ hw('B') }} or {{ hw('E') }} to match the device's thread, or leave it out; see {{ ref('bay-devices', 'Fit 5.25-inch devices or the filler blank') }}.

A single 120 mm radiator mounts in the same position; radiators up to 277 mm long fit. Four-fan push-pull is not officially supported.</p>
