The rear has mounting for one 120 mm fan up to 38 mm thick, on a removable bracket.

### 1. Remove the rear fan bracket

Remove the {{ hw('A') }} securing the rear fan bracket and lift the bracket out.

### 2. Mount the fan on the bracket

Secure the fan to the bracket with the self-tapping screws supplied with the fan. For a liquid cooler, see {{ ref('aio-installation', 'Install a rear AIO radiator') }}.

### 3. Refit the bracket

Refit the bracket and secure it with its {{ hw('A') }}.
