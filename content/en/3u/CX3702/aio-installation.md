The rear fan position takes one 120 mm AIO radiator (Asetek 650LKS, up to 27 mm thick), or a 92 mm AIO (Asetek 645LT) on a 3D-printed 120-to-92 mm adapter.

### 1. Position the fan and radiator

Place the 120 mm fan against the radiator, as shown. Use the screws specified by the cooler manufacturer.

### 2. Mount the assembly

Mount the radiator and fan on the rear fan bracket, then refit the bracket with its {{ hw('A') }}. Route the tubes clear of the PSU, GPU and drive cables.
