### 1. Route and secure cables

Route front-panel, PCIe, power, and fan cables along the front edge of the motherboard tray's cable-management bar. Its location is identified in {{ ref("dimensions", "the top-view clearance drawing") }}. Bundle excess slack behind the PSU and secure it with ties.

Keep all cables clear of fan blades, the drive-bay adapters, and the tray's engagement points. Release ties that anchor cables to the fixed chassis before removing the tray.

### 2. Close the chassis

Refit the filter and faceplate, aligning their locating pins. Align the lid flanges with the chassis slots, lower the lid, and slide it forward. Secure the lid with its two {{ hw('A') }}.
