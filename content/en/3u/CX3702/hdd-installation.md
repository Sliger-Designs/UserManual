The ten 3.5-inch hot-swap bays are behind the magnetic faceplate. Each bay connects through a preinstalled SFF-8482 SAS/SATA to SATA adapter.

### 1. Open the drive bays

Unlock the drive retention tray with the {{ hw('N') }} and slide the tray back.

### 2. Fit the shoulder screws

Install four {{ hw('J') }} in the side mounting holes of each 3.5-inch HDD.

### 3. Insert the drives

Slide each drive into a bay, guiding the shoulder screws through the slots, until it seats in the adapter.
