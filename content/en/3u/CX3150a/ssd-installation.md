### 1. Position the drive

One 2.5-inch SSD up to 15 mm thick fits between the power supply and motherboard I/O shield.

Position the drive with its connectors facing upward and its underside facing the power supply.

### 2. Secure the drive

Install two {{ hw("B") }} through the tray into the drive. Tighten by hand without forcing the threads.

<p class="info-note" role="note">For M.2 storage, follow the motherboard's installation instructions.</p>
