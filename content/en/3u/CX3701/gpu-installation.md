### 1. Prepare the slot

Select the PCIe slot specified by your motherboard manual. Remove the corresponding chassis slot covers and open the motherboard slot latch, if present.

### 2. Seat the card

Align the card with the motherboard slot and rear bracket opening. Lower it carefully and apply gentle, even pressure until fully seated.

### 3. Secure the bracket

Fasten the card's mounting bracket to the tray with {{ hw("A") }}. Fit {{ hw("G") }} over unused openings and secure them with {{ hw("A") }}.

<p class="info-note" role="note">Cards up to {{ facts.gpu_length_baseline_mm }} mm long, including the power connector, and {{ facts.gpu_height_max_mm }} mm tall. Leave clearance for 12-pin 12VHPWR / CEM5 power connectors. With a dual-slot card, the side vent fan must be a slim (15 mm thick) 120 mm fan.</p>
