Four 2.5-inch SSD mounting points, for drives up to 7 mm thick, are located behind the power button and front USB area.

### 1. Position the SSDs

Align each SSD with its mounting points behind the front I/O.

### 2. Secure the drives

Secure each drive with two {{ hw('B') }}. Route the cables clear of the fans and drive bays.

<p class="info-note" role="note">For M.2 storage, follow the motherboard's installation instructions.</p>
