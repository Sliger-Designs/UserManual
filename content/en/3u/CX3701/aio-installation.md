The CX3701 can take one 92 mm AIO cooler (Asetek 645LT) at the rear fan position, using a 3D-printed adapter from the 120 mm fan mounting to the 92 mm radiator.

### 1. Fit the adapter

Mount the 120-to-92 mm adapter on the rear fan bracket.

### 2. Mount the radiator and fan

Secure the 92 mm radiator and its fan to the adapter with the screws specified by the cooler manufacturer, then refit the bracket with its {{ hw('A') }}.

<p class="info-note" role="note">This configuration is not officially supported and may interfere with the motherboard tray or other components. Check every clearance before installation.</p>
