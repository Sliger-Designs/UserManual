The side vent has mounting for one 120 mm fan up to 38 mm thick, on a removable bracket.

### 1. Remove the side fan bracket

Remove the {{ hw('E') }} securing the side fan bracket and lift the bracket out.

### 2. Mount the fan on the bracket

Secure the fan to the bracket with the self-tapping screws supplied with the fan.

### 3. Refit the bracket

Refit the bracket and secure it with its {{ hw('E') }}.

<p class="info-note" role="note">With a dual-slot PCIe card such as a GPU, the side vent fan must be a slim (15 mm thick) 120 mm fan.</p>
