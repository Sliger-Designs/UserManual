The front drive cage takes two 3.5-inch drives up to 25 mm thick, or up to seven 7 mm 2.5-inch SSDs. One SSD mounts above the 3.5-inch drives (up to 15 mm thick) and one below them (7 mm).

### Mount the drives

Fasten 3.5-inch drives with {{ hw('A') }} and 2.5-inch SSDs with {{ hw('D') }} through the cage side walls.
