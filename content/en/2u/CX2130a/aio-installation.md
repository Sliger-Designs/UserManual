### 1. Assemble the cooler

Fit the four 80 mm fans and fan guards supplied with the Dynatron L34 to its radiator, following the cooler instructions.

### 2. Mount the radiator

Mount the radiator and fan assembly on the front fan panel in place of the front fans, using the screws supplied with the cooler. Route the tubing to the CPU block clear of the PCIe slots.

### Check component clearance

The L34 stack (radiator, fans and fan guards) is 86.5 mm thick, leaving **{{ facts.gpu_length_fans_aio_mm }} mm** for the GPU.
