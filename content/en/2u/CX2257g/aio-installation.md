The Dynatron L34 2U AIO (four 80 mm fans, 320 mm radiator) mounts on the fan bracket in place of the fans, with its four 80 mm fans between the fan bracket and the radiator.

### 1. Remove the fan guards

Remove all the screws holding the fans and fan guards to the radiator, leaving the fans on the radiator. Set the fan guards aside; they are not used in the chassis. Keep the screws.

### 2. Mount the radiator

Hold the radiator and fans against the inside of the fan bracket, fans toward the front. Secure them through the fan bracket with half of the radiator screws, passing each screw through a fan into the radiator. Route the tubing to the CPU block clear of the PCIe slots.

### Check component clearance
