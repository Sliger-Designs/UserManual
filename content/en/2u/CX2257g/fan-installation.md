### 1. Align the fans

With the fan bracket out of the chassis, align four 80 mm fans with it, airflow toward the motherboard. Each fan uses four mounting points.

### 2. Secure the fans

Use the screws supplied with your fans. Pass them through the bracket and into the fan frames.

Tighten sufficiently to prevent movement and vibration.
