The front drive cage takes two 3.5-inch drives up to 25 mm thick, one SSD above them (up to 15 mm thick) and one below them (7 mm).

### 1. Mount the drives

Fasten 3.5-inch drives with {{ hw('A') }} and 2.5-inch SSDs with {{ hw('D') }} through the cage side walls.

### 2. Check the filled cage

Confirm that every drive is secured and that the connectors face the rear of the chassis.
