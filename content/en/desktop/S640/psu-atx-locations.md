### ATX positions

The views show alternative ATX bracket locations and orientations. An ATX PSU at the rear of S640 limits the motherboard to ATX. EATX is supported with the PSU at the front or on the side fan bracket.

Secure the bracket to the chassis with four green-marked screws **D**. Check the actual motherboard dimensions, GPU clearance, the fan/radiator stack, storage brackets and connector access.

Check cable reach and confirm that exterior panels close without loading a cable or component before tightening.
