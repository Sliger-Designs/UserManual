The bay holds either the filler blank or a 5.25-inch device. The filler blank provides mounting for two 2.5-inch SSDs and one 3.5-inch HDD.

### 1. Remove the filler blank

To fit a 5.25-inch device, slide the filler blank out of the bracket.

### 2. Install the 5.25-inch device

Slide the device into the bracket. Align its mounting holes with the bracket and secure it with {{ hw('D') }} on both sides. A 5.25-inch to 3.5-inch adapter is available separately.

### 3. Or keep the filler blank

Without a 5.25-inch device, refit the filler blank to the bracket with {{ hw('A') }}. Mount 2.5-inch SSDs and a 3.5-inch HDD on the blank as needed.
