Four 2.5-inch SSD mounting points are located behind the power button and front USB area.

### 1. Position the SSDs

Align each 2.5-inch SSD with its mounting points behind the front I/O.

### 2. Secure the drives

Secure each drive with two {{ hw('B') }}. Route cables clear of the fan bracket.

The backplane has two SATA data ports near these mounts. Short cables (about 6 inches) can reach two SSDs. See {{ ref('backplane-connections', 'Connect the backplane') }} for data and power connections.
