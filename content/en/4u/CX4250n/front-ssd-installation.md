Four 2.5-inch SSD mounting points are located behind the power button and front USB area.

### 1. Position the SSDs

Align each 2.5-inch SSD with its mounting points behind the front I/O.

### 2. Secure the drives

Secure each drive with two {{ hw('B') }}. Route cables clear of the fan bracket.

<p class="info-note" role="note">The backplane has two SATA ports on the edge nearest these mounting points, so short (about 6-inch) SATA cables can connect two SSDs. Their data runs through the backplane's SFF-8643 connectors to the HBA or motherboard.</p>
