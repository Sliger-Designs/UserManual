### 1. Close the retention tray

Slide the drive retention tray forward over the installed drives.

### 2. Lock the tray

Secure the tray with the {{ hw('K') }} and lock it with the {{ hw('M') }}.

### 3. Connect the backplane data

Connect SFF-8643 (Mini-SAS HD) data cables (not included) from the HBA, RAID card or motherboard to the backplane's data connectors.

### 4. Connect the backplane power

Power the backplane from the PSU with the {{ hw('L') }}: the dual 4-pin cable to an EPS plug, and each single 4-pin cable to a 6-pin PCIe plug.

<p class="info-note" role="note">Hot-swap support depends on the HBA or RAID card, motherboard and operating system. A SAS controller can run SATA drives; a SATA controller cannot run SAS drives.</p>
