### 1. Position the fans

Place three 120 mm fans between the fan bracket and the 360 mm radiator. Remove any fan screws occupying the radiator mounting positions.

### 2. Align and secure the assembly

Align all twelve mounting points in the bracket, fans and radiator. Use the cooler manufacturer's specified radiator screws through the bracket and fan frames into the radiator.
