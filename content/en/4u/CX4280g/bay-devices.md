### 1. Fit a 5.25-inch device

With the bay bank out of the chassis, remove the existing device or filler from the chosen slot. Slide the new device into the bank, align its mounting holes, and secure it on both sides with {{ hw('D') }} (<span style="white-space: nowrap">217-477P</span>).

### 2. Or fit a bay drive cage

Secure the cage through its side mounting holes with {{ hw('D') }} (<span style="white-space: nowrap">217-477P</span>). Follow the cage manufacturer's instructions for fitting drives and connecting power and data.

### 3. Or fit a filler blank

Slide the blank into the open slot and secure it. See {{ ref('bay-blank-drives', 'Mount drives on a filler blank') }} for drive mounting.

Lower the bank back into the chassis, slide it toward the subfront, and reinstall its four {{ hw('A') }}. Reconnect any cables and refit the faceplate.
