### Check the radiator end and tube fittings

The two illustrations show a conflicting arrangement and an arrangement with clearance. Check the radiator end tank, tube fittings, drive cage, and front I/O assembly together before fastening.

### Check radiator thickness

Radiators thicker than 27 mm can interfere with the internal drive cage. Verify the complete fan/radiator thickness and end-tank geometry for the selected cooler.

### Preserve the required clearance

If parts touch or prevent the drive cage from seating, change the configuration or omit the cage. Do not force the cage against the radiator or tubing.

The source draft's exact fan-position restriction with the cage still needs technical review; do not assume every three-fan AIO fits with the cage.
