### 1. Position the SSD

Align a 2.5-inch SSD up to 15 mm thick with the tray mounting location shown. Keep its connectors accessible and check clearance to the PSU and motherboard.

### 2. Secure the drive

Install two {{ hw('B') }} through the tray into the drive. Tighten by hand without forcing the threads.

For drive-cage capacities, see {{ ref("drive-cage-layouts-1", "Drive-cage layout examples") }}. Do not add tray mounting positions to the listed cage capacities; simultaneous use needs a verified configuration.

For M.2 storage, follow the motherboard's installation instructions.
