### 1. Position the ATX power supply

Place the ATX PSU into the mounting location shown on the motherboard tray. Align its rear mounting holes and keep the fan intake clear.

### 2. Secure the PSU

Install {{ hw('A') }} through the tray into the PSU. Tighten by hand and route cables clear of tray engagement points.
