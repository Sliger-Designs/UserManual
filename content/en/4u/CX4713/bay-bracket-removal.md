Removing the 5.25-inch bay bracket gives access to its filler blanks and makes motherboard installation easier.

### 1. Remove the bracket screws

Remove the {{ hw('E') }} that secure the bay bracket to the chassis.

### 2. Slide the bracket back

Slide the bracket toward the rear of the chassis until it clears the front panel.

### 3. Lift the bracket out

Lift the bracket up and out of the chassis. Disconnect drive cables first if drives are already installed.
