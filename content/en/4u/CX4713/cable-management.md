### 1. Route and secure cables

Run drive, front-panel, PCIe, power and fan cables along the front edge of the motherboard tray's cable-management bar, highlighted in green. Keep them clear of the fan bracket and fan blades.

Bundle excess slack behind the PSU and secure it with ties. Release any ties that anchor cables to the fixed chassis before removing the tray again.

### 2. Close the chassis

Refit the magnetic faceplate. Align the lid flanges with the chassis slots, lower the lid, and slide it forward. Secure the lid with its two {{ hw('A') }}.
