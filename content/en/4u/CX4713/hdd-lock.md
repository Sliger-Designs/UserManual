### 1. Close the retention tray

Slide the drive retention tray forward over the installed drives.

### 2. Lock the tray

Secure the tray with the {{ hw('K') }} and lock it with the {{ hw('N') }}.

### Connect the drives

Connect SATA data cables (not included) to the adapters inside the chassis, and power them with the {{ hw('M') }}.

<p class="info-note" role="note">Hot-swap support depends on the HBA or RAID card, motherboard and operating system. A SAS controller can run SATA drives; a SATA controller cannot run SAS drives.</p>
