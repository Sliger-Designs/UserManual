### 1. Choose the drive layout

The illustration is one populated example, not a promise that every drive mount can be used at the same time. Confirmed cage layouts include **three 3.5-inch HDDs**, **three HDDs + one 7 mm SSD**, **two HDDs + three 7 mm SSDs**, and **eight 7 mm SSDs**. Nine SSDs fit only when interlocked so that none sit flat.

### 2. Align the drive mounting holes

Position drives so their connectors can be reached after the cage is installed. Match the cage holes to the drive mounting holes.

### 3. Secure the drives

The populated example identifies {{ hw('A') }} and {{ hw('D') }}. Match the fastener to the drive's thread and permitted screw engagement; do not force mismatched screws.
