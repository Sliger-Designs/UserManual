### 1. Orient the radiator correctly

Fit the radiator with its end tank and tube fittings at the end away from the drive cage, as shown. The fittings then clear the cage and the front I/O assembly.

### 2. Avoid the reversed orientation

With the end tank at the drive-cage end, the tank sits against the cage and the cage cannot seat. Turn the radiator around instead.

### Check radiator thickness

Radiators thicker than 27 mm can interfere with the internal drive cage. Verify the complete fan and radiator thickness for the selected cooler. Do not force the cage against the radiator or tubing.
