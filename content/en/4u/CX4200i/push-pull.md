### 1. Choose the configuration

Push-pull uses a second fan bank behind the radiator. A **240 mm radiator with four 120 mm fans** fits with the internal drive cage in place. A **360 mm radiator with six 120 mm fans** does not: remove the drive cage first.

### 2. Attach the second fan bank

Align the additional fans with the rear of the radiator and secure them using the radiator manufacturer's specified screws.

### 3. Mount the complete assembly

Align the front fan bank, radiator, and mounting panel. Install the specified screws through the panel and front fans into the radiator.

The assembly must clear the motherboard tray, front I/O, the drive cage (if fitted) and all installed components. With either push-pull assembly and 25 mm fans, maximum GPU length is **{{ facts.gpu_length_max_cooling_mm }} mm**.
