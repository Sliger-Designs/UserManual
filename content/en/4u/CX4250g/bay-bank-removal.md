Remove the lid and magnetic faceplate. Disconnect the cables from any devices in the bank you are removing.

### 1. Remove the four screws

Remove the four {{ hw('A') }} (<span style="white-space: nowrap">217-499</span>) that secure the selected bank to the subfront.

### 2. Slide the bank back

Push the whole bay bank toward the rear of the chassis until it clears the subfront.

### 3. Lift the bank out

Lift the bank straight up and out. Set it on a stable work surface before changing any device or filler.
