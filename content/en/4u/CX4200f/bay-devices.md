Each bay holds either a filler blank or a 5.25-inch device. Each filler blank provides mounting for two 2.5-inch SSDs and one 3.5-inch HDD.

### 1. Remove the filler blanks

For each bay that will hold a 5.25-inch device, remove the {{ hw('A') }} securing its filler blank to the bracket, then slide the blank out of the front of the bracket.

### 2. Install the 5.25-inch devices

Slide each device into the bracket from the front. Align its mounting holes with the bracket and secure it on both sides with screws that match the device's thread: {{ hw('D') }} or {{ hw('B') }} for M3, {{ hw('A') }} for 6-32. A 5.25-inch to 3.5-inch adapter is available separately.

### 3. Keep the blanks in unused bays

Leave the filler blank fitted in any bay without a device, secured with its {{ hw('A') }}. To mount drives on a blank, see {{ ref('bay-blank-drives', 'Mount drives on a filler blank') }}.
