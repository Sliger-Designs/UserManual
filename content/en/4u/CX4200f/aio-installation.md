### 1. Position the fans

Place two 120 mm fans between the fan bracket and the 240 mm radiator. Remove any fan screws occupying the radiator mounting positions.

### 2. Align and secure the assembly

Align all eight mounting points in the bracket, fans, and radiator. Use the cooler manufacturer's specified radiator screws through the bracket and fan frames into the radiator.

Incorrect screw length can damage the radiator. Check that the radiator clears the front I/O assembly and the 5.25-inch bay bracket before tightening.

### Check component clearance

With the specified Asetek 240 mm AIO and two 25 mm fans, maximum GPU length is **{{ facts.gpu_length_fans_aio_mm }} mm**. A single 120 mm radiator mounts in the same positions.
