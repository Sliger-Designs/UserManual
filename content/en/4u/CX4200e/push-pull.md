### 1. Attach the second fan bank

A 240 mm radiator may use two 120 mm fans on each side, four in total. Align the additional fans with the rear of the radiator and secure them using the radiator manufacturer's specified screws.

### 2. Mount the complete assembly

Align the front fan bank, radiator, and subfront. Install the specified screws through the subfront and front fans into the radiator.
