# CX4170a 2.5-inch SSD capacity — November 4, 2026

The listed capacity is **ten 7 mm 2.5-inch SSDs**: eight in the internal
drive cage and two on the motherboard tray. This replaces the 2026-10-27 record of nine
as the highest cage count.

The cage can physically hold nine SSDs (layout example 5, `9xSSD.png`), but only with the
drives interlocked ("laced") together so that none sit flat. The manual keeps that
illustration, described as technically possible, and does not count it toward the
listed capacity. Eight (example 4, `8xSSD.png`) is the official cage capacity.

The two tray positions follow the mounting shared by all 4U trays (two 7 mm SSDs or one
15 mm SSD, unusable with the rear fan bracket); see `2026-11-04-4U-tray-ssd-mounting.md`.

Drive counts should generally lead with total mounting locations, with
how many can be used at the same time given as secondary information.

Applied in `products/overrides.json`, `products/CX4170a-drive-layouts.json` and the
CX4170a specification and drive-cage pages.
