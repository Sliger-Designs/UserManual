# CX3150n and CX3180n (3U backplane NAS) — November 23, 2026

The CX3150n and CX3180n are the CX3701 (15-inch, Mini-ITX) and CX3702 (18-inch, MicroATX) with the
ten-port SAS/SATA backplane instead of ten SFF-8482 adapters, as the CX4250n and CX4280n are to the
CX4712 and CX4713. Their rows in the private import files (`sources/original/compatibility-list.csv`
and `bulk-create.csv`, git-ignored) are copies of the CX3701 and CX3702 rows, and their supplemental
and override facts are copied from those models. The product records therefore match the base
models fact for fact.

Basis:

- Website specifications (`sources/web/cx3150n-specs.txt`, `cx3180n-specs.txt`, retrieved
  2026-11-20). The CX3150n page matches the CX3701 page apart from the storage wiring, the DAS
  cable list, and the weight and outer-dimension lines it leaves out.
- The NAS kit is the backplane kit (`products/shared/CX-NAS-HWKIT-backplane.json`): J shoulder
  screws, K thumb screws, L backplane power cables, M key pair.
- The connection pages are the shared 4U n pages (`backplane-connections.md`,
  `backplane-power-cables.md`); the website describes the same ten-port board and cables.

Illustrations: the CX3701 and CX3702 renders are reused for every step (same chassis; none of the
views shows the backplane). The CX3180n exploded view was first the CX3180n render supplied 2026-11-10;
Sliger preferred the newer CX3150n angle, so it was re-rendered to match (2026-11-23). The CX3150n exploded view is a new Solid Edge render of `CX3150n.asm` (lid up, faceplate
forward; camera framed by Sliger, 2026-11-23). The covers use the CX3701 and CX3702 product photos.

## Decided 2026-11-23

- CX3180n GPU clearance is 261 mm (total internal depth; GPU length plus power connector). It
  differs from the CX3702.
- CX3180n motherboards are as the CX3702: Mini-ITX, Deep Mini-ITX and MicroATX, not Deep MicroATX.
  The website listed Deep MicroATX by mistake and has been corrected (snapshot refreshed
  2026-11-23).
- The CX3180n has no AIO support. The space gives the motherboard more clearance to the side fan
  and more cable room behind the PSU. The guide has no AIO page and the import rows have no AIO
  compatibility.
- The 3U backplane is the same board as the 4U one. Both 3U n guides use the 4U backplane render
  (`_CX4250n_BACKPLANE.jpg`) and its callouts.

## Renders from the _INT-MIN models (2026-11-25)

Every CX3150n and CX3180n step is rendered from `CX3150n_INT-MIN` and `CX3180n_INT-MIN` (same trays as the CX3150g and
CX3180g; the CX3180n uses standoffs). The exploded views show the tray out the back, with a "Motherboard tray" callout.

- The four front SSDs fit with the drive retention plate in place; their screws are reached from behind the bracket and
  through its slot.
- The retention plate slides back to open the bays and forward to lock; the key lock's tab then sits in the plate's slot.
  One key-lock close-up is shared by both guides.
- The backplane render carries the J13, J7, J1, power-input and SATA callouts (CX4250n labels).
- HDD step 2 reuses the CX4250n render (four shoulder screws) until the SA-3000n demo drives get all four.
