# CX3152a / CX3152i — November 5, 2026

The CX3152 models are the 15-inch 3U chassis with an ATX power supply on the motherboard tray.
The PSU overlaps the edge of SSI-CEB, EATX and ATX motherboards, which limits them to five
PCIe slots; some low-profile USB-header adapters may be needed (website; the drawings show
19.6 mm between the motherboard and the bottom of the PSU).

## PCIe slots

Confirmed: five **full-height** slots, cards up to 115 mm tall including power connectors
(website, import sheet, rear render). This replaces the compatibility CSV's "5x Half Height",
which the spec review had approved only as a reference value.

## Storage

Confirmed: the tray has two 2.5-inch mounting locations, one SSD flat under the PSU (render 7a)
and one upright beside the motherboard (7b). Every 3U "i" chassis has the same 5.25-inch bay
bracket, so the CX3152i counts as the CX3150i (`2026-11-05-CX3150-storage-and-cooling.md`),
with the shared filler blank (`2026-11-05-bay-filler-blank-shared.md`).

| Model | 2.5-inch locations | 3.5-inch locations |
|---|---|---|
| CX3152a | 2 (tray: under the PSU 1, beside the motherboard 1) | 0 |
| CX3152i | 9 (tray 2 + filler blank 4 + bay bracket top 2 + under the bay bracket 1) | 2 (filler blank 1 + bay bracket top 1) |

The CX3152i total replaces the spec review's 2. The drawings' note "SSDs can be placed in any
of the four locations shown" is not repeated in the manuals; the tray total stays two.

## Cooling

- CX3152a: three front fans; one 360 mm, one 240 mm or two 120 mm radiators. Six-fan push-pull
  is not officially supported (as CX3150a/CX3151a); renders 3d and 3e are not used.
- CX3152i: two front fans; one 240 mm or one 120 mm radiator up to 277 mm long. A radiator can
  touch the screws retaining a 5.25-inch device (top-view drawing note): countersunk B or E
  screws there, or leave them out, as on the CX3150i.

## PSU

ATX, maximum length including connectors 270 mm (CX3152a) and 268 mm (CX3152i), as approved in
the spec review.
