# CX4250n (25-inch 4U backplane NAS) — November 20, 2026

The CX4250n is a website concept model and is not in the catalog CSVs. Its product record
(`products/CX4250n.json`) starts from the CX4712 facts.

Basis:

- Same chassis as the CX4712: 25 × 17 × 6.96 inches, two 5.25-inch bays, ten 3.5-inch hot-swap
  bays behind a magnetic faceplate, the same tray, fan wall, PSU, GPU and SSD limits. The
  website specifications (`sources/web/cx4250n-specs.txt`, retrieved 2026-11-20) match the
  CX4712 page line for line apart from the storage wiring, a new Direct Attached Storage
  section, and the weight and outer-dimension lines, which the CX4250n page leaves out.
- The 3U and 4U n and g series share chassis wraps and front panels (confirmed 2026-11-20).

Storage wiring (website, 2026-11-20), the CX4250n's difference from the CX4712:

- One ten-port SATA/SAS PCB backplane, preinstalled, with SFF-8643 (Mini-SAS HD) data
  connectors, instead of ten individual SATA/SAS adapters.
- Three backplane power cables: one dual 4-pin (board side) to one EPS plug (PSU side), and two
  single 4-pin (board side) to one 6-pin PCIe plug (PSU side). No data cables are included.
- Two SATA ports on the backplane edge nearest the four front SSD mounts, for short (~6 inch)
  SATA cables; their data runs through the SFF-8643 connectors.
- Four shoulder screws per drive guide it to the backplane connector.

Renders: new Solid Edge renders of the CX4250n model (2026-11-20). Steps whose view does not
show the backplane or cage reuse the CX4712 images (fan wall 3a–3e, I/O shield, PSU, GPU, tray
SSD, bay devices, dimension drawings). The cover uses the CX4250g product photo (identical
from the outside).

Open:
