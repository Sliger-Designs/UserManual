# CX2151a, CX2151c and CX2151x (15-inch 2U, riser GPU) — November 12, 2026

Values from the 2026-11-04 lightning round (private workbook, Review Queue) and the website
specification tabs (`sources/web/cx2151{a,c,x}-specs.txt`).

- Outer dimensions 381 × 431.8 × 87.88 mm (15 × 17 × 3.46 inches); the bulk-create 330.2 mm
  depth was copied from the CX2130.
- GPU length: 353 mm without front fans (confirmed no-fan internal depth for the 15-inch 2U;
  the compatibility CSV 349 mm is low); 327 mm with 25 mm fans; 314 mm with 38 mm fans.
- CX2151a: four fan positions; AIO = Dynatron L34 (86.5 mm stack), 266 mm GPU. CX2151c and
  CX2151x: three fan positions, AIO not officially supported (the three-fan Dynatron L35
  interferes with the brackets).
- One GPU on a PCIe x16 riser in two full-height horizontal slots: up to 2.5 slots (50 mm)
  wide and 120 mm tall including power connectors; a rear GPU support bracket holds the card.
  Four slot covers (bulk-create).
- SFX or SFX-L power supply. Motherboards: Mini-ITX, Deep Mini-ITX, MicroATX, Deep MicroATX.
- Storage: CX2151a has no drive mounting positions, NVMe only (confirmed 2026-11-12); CX2151c a front drive cage behind
  two external 3.5-inch adapter bays (two 3.5-inch drives up to 25 mm, one SSD above up to 15 mm,
  one below 7 mm); CX2151x an internal drive cage (two 3.5-inch drives, or up to seven 7 mm SSDs).
- Renders: CX2151a and CX2151c numbered sets. The CX2151a folder's front render is an identical
  copy named `_CX2177a_FRONT.tif` and is used as the CX2151a front (confirmed 2026-11-12). The CX2151x set is
  unnumbered and has no front render (grey placeholder). CX2151x clearance drawings:
  `_CX2151x_ADVANCED_TOP VIEW.webp` and `_CX2151x_ADVANCED_FRONT VIEW.webp` (supplied 2026-11-12).
- Exploded views: the REV20261112 views (supplied 2026-11-12) show the rack ears and pull handles,
  so each part key ends with one "Rack ear & pull handle" callout.
- AIO (CX2151a): the shared Dynatron L34 renders (`illustrations/common/2u-l34-install/`,
  supplied 2026-11-13) and the shared 2U AIO page replace the grey placeholder.
