# CX2137a and CX2137b (13-inch 2U) — November 12, 2026

Values from the 2026-11-04 lightning round (private workbook, Review Queue) and the website
specification tabs (`sources/web/cx2137{a,b}-specs.txt`).

- Outer dimensions 330.2 × 431.8 × 87.88 mm (13 × 17 × 3.46 inches).
- GPU length: 302 mm without front fans; 275 mm with 25 mm fans (lower of the 1 mm-apart
  values; website 276 mm). With 38 mm fans: 264 mm (302 - 38; confirmed 2026-11-12, superseding
  the website's 263 mm).
- CX2137a: four fan positions; AIO = Dynatron L34 (86.5 mm stack), 215 mm GPU. SSI-CEB boards
  and EATX boards over 10.25 inches deep are incompatible with 38 mm fans (website).
- CX2137b: three fan positions; no AIO support (confirmed 2026-11-12).
- FlexATX power supply up to 200 mm (not ATX).
- Motherboards up to EATX / SSI-CEB 12 × 10.5 inches. Seven half-height slots, 72 mm cards.
- Storage: the same rear drive bracket as the CX2177a: five 7 mm 2.5-inch SSDs when the 3.5-inch
  mounts are unused; two 3.5-inch drives up to 20 mm; or one 3.5-inch HDD and one 2.5-inch SSD
  up to 15 mm.
- Renders: the CX2137b exploded view is `_CX2137b.jpg`. No exploded view was in the CX2137a
  folder on 2026-11-12, so that page is a grey placeholder. The missing CX2137a AIO render is a
  grey placeholder, as for the CX2177 family; 2U cable-management pages are text only.
