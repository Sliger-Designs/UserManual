# CX3151a / CX3151i / CX3151x — November 5, 2026

The CX3151 models are the CX3150 chassis with a horizontal riser GPU: three full-height
horizontal PCIe slots with one PCIe 5.0 x16 riser, cards up to 150 mm tall and 72 mm
(3.5 slots) wide. As of 2025-03-22 the single vertical PCIe slot is no longer fitted.

## Storage: same as the CX3150 family

Confirmed: mounting locations are counted as for the CX3150 models
(`2026-11-05-CX3150-storage-and-cooling.md`). The tray takes two SSDs up to 7 mm thick or one
up to 15 mm; the CX3151i has the same 5.25-inch bay bracket as the CX3150i; the filler blank
is the shared part (`2026-11-05-bay-filler-blank-shared.md`).

| Model | 2.5-inch locations | 3.5-inch locations |
|---|---|---|
| CX3151a | 2 (tray) | 0 |
| CX3151i | 9 (tray 2 + filler blank 4 + bay bracket top 2 + under the bay bracket 1) | 2 (filler blank 1 + bay bracket top 1) |
| CX3151x | 10 (tray 2 + drive bracket 8) | 3 (drive bracket) |

These replace the spec review's 1, 2 and 8 2.5-inch locations.

## CX3151-GPUKIT

A separately packaged kit, listed after SBAG-HWKIT and items G–I, as for CX-NAS-HWKIT:

- J: GPU support braces (3D printed), one short and one tall. The short brace (27.9 mm,
  labelled "GPU WITH BACKPLATE" in the renders) is for GPUs with a backplate; the tall brace
  (31.8 mm, "GPU WITHOUT BACKPLATE") for GPUs without one.
- K: 217-349 screws, 6-32 × 1 inch, two (teal in the renders), securing the brace to the tray.

Before fitting a brace, two yellow A screws are removed from the tray at its position. Renders
of the braces (`CX3151_GPUBraces.jpg`) and the 217-349 screw were supplied the same day in
`illustrations/common/hardware/`.

## Fasteners shown in other colours

- Rear cover over the riser slots: yellow A (6-32 hex). The renders show it purple on removal
  and green on refitting.

## Render reuse

- CX3151i standoff, I/O-shield, motherboard, PSU and SSD steps use the CX3151x renders (same
  riser tray and rear). The CX3150i-named copies in the CX3151i folder show the eight-slot
  CX3150i rear and are not used.
- CX3151i bay-bracket drive steps use the CX3150i renders 4j, 4k and 4h + SSD (same bracket).
- CX3151x GPU-brace step for GPUs without a backplate uses `10b_CX3151i_GPU SUPPORT NO
  BACKPLATE.jpg`, supplied in the CX3151x folder.
- CX3151a: the front-ports page uses the shared 3U "a" front drawing, as on the CX3150a (the
  folder's `_CX3172a_FRONT.tif` shows the same front). The cover is `_CX3150a_rendering.jpg` from
  the CX3151a folder, which differs from the CX3150a file.

## Not used

- "Alternate GPU setup" renders (CX3151a 9f, CX3151i 11d) and the two-card clearance drawings:
  they show the removed vertical slot. The manuals use the "ONE GPU" drawings.
- Push-pull renders (CX3151a 3d, 3e): push-pull is not officially supported.
- The 28 CX3152a renders supplied in the CX3151x folder were moved to `illustrations/3u/CX3152a/`
  (2026-11-05) for the CX3152 family.
