# CX2257g (25-inch 2U, four 5.25-inch bays) — November 23, 2026

Website specifications: `sources/web/cx2257g-specs.txt` (retrieved 2026-11-20, refreshed
2026-11-23 after Sliger corrected the 5.25-inch listing to four bays with two filler blanks).

Decided by Sliger on 2026-11-23:

- 25 inches deep (the website page lists no outer dimensions). Width and height are as the other
  2U chassis: 635 × 431.8 × 87.88 mm.
- The CX2177a pages are the base for the 2U procedures (lid, rack ears, 80 mm fans, Dynatron L34
  AIO, rear drive cage, motherboard, PSU, GPU). The four 80 mm fans mount on a lift-out fan bracket
  (as the 25-inch 3U g), so the guide adds "Remove the fan bracket" and "Reinstall the fan bracket".
- Illustrations: new Solid Edge renders of `CX2257g_INT` (cameras framed by Sliger, 2026-11-23). Sliger
  added the fans and their 16 screws, a half-height card, the drives, the second lid screw and the drive
  bracket's side screw first. Reused from the CX2177a: the SSD and HDD into the drive bracket (the same
  CX200-712B bracket) and the I/O shield. The L34 page has its own renders of the Dynatron L34 in the
  model (fans, radiator and pump black, as in the CX2177a renders). Grey boxes: the top- and front-view
  clearance drawings.
- Four 5.25-inch bays in two columns of two. Two filler blanks are included. Banks come out as on
  the 4U g (four 217-499 screws, slide back, lift out), and devices fix with 217-477P.
- Storage counts what ships: rear drive cage (five 7 mm SSDs or two 3.5-inch HDDs) plus two filler
  blanks give 13 × 2.5-inch SSD or 4 × 3.5-inch HDD mounting locations.

From the website: four 80 mm mid-chassis fans up to 38 mm thick; Dynatron L34 (320 mm, 4 × 80 mm)
AIO; FlexATX PSU up to 210 mm; seven half-height slots, cards up to 72 mm; GPU length 368 mm
without fans, 342 mm with 25 mm fans and 330 mm with 38 mm fans; CPU coolers up to 66 mm;
Mini-ITX, Deep Mini-ITX, FlexATX, MicroATX, Deep MicroATX, ATX, EATX and SSI-CEB (12 × 10.5 inches);
front I/O is the power switch and two USB-A.

## Decided later on 2026-11-23

- The Dynatron L34 fits the CX2257g (and would fit a CX2250g). It mounts on the mid-chassis fan
  wall behind the bays.
- GPU length with the L34: 368.2 mm usable depth less the 86.5 mm L34 stack (radiator and fans) is
  281.7 mm, rounded down to 281 mm. This is the same method as the CX2177a (404 − 86.5 → 317 mm).
