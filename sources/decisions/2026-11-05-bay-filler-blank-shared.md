# 5.25-inch bay filler blank: one shared part — November 5, 2026

Confirmed: the 5.25-inch bay filler blank is one part, shared by every chassis with a 5.25-inch
bay (3U and 4U). Its capacity is the same everywhere: four 2.5-inch SSDs, or one 3.5-inch HDD
and two 2.5-inch SSDs (`2026-11-04-bay-filler-blank-capacity.md`). Two 2.5-inch SSDs up to
15 mm thick can also mount through its bottom holes, as rendered for the CX3150i (4f).

Applied:

- One page, `content/en/shared/rackmount/bay-blank-drives.md`, replaces the identical copies in
  the CX4150e, CX4150i, CX4170e, CX4170i, CX4200e, CX4200f, CX4200i, CX4712 and CX4713 folders
  and the CX3150i page, and adds the 15 mm step to all of them.
- Every manual shows the same three renders from `illustrations/common/4u-bay-blank/`:
  `drive12.jpg.jpg` (four SSDs; byte-identical to the CX3150i 4e), the CX3150i 4f render
  (two 15 mm SSDs), and `drive6.1.jpg.jpg` (HDD with green E screws).

Mounting-location totals are unchanged: they already count four 2.5-inch and one 3.5-inch
location per blank.
