# CX2130a, CX2130c and CX2130x (13-inch 2U, SFX) — November 12, 2026

Values from the 2026-11-04 lightning round (private workbook, Review Queue) and the website
specification tabs (`sources/web/cx2130{a,c,x}-specs.txt`).

- Outer dimensions 330.2 × 431.8 × 87.88 mm (13 × 17 × 3.46 inches); five half-height slots,
  72 mm cards; seven slot covers (bulk-create); SFX or SFX-L power supply; Mini-ITX to Deep
  MicroATX boards.
- GPU length: 302 mm without fans and 275 mm with 25 mm fans (review); 264 mm with 38 mm fans
  (302 - 38; confirmed 2026-11-12, superseding the website's 263 mm).
- CX2130a: four fan positions; one 320 mm radiator, the Dynatron L34 (tested and confirmed to
  fit, website), 215 mm GPU (review). Storage: one M.2/NVMe drive only.
- CX2130c and CX2130x: three fan positions; AIO not officially supported (confirmed 2026-11-12).
- CX2130c: front drive cage behind two external 3.5-inch adapter bays (two 3.5-inch drives up
  to 25 mm, one SSD above up to 15 mm, one below 7 mm; one more SSD per optional filler blank).
- CX2130x: internal drive cage with two 3.5-inch drives, or up to seven 7 mm SSDs when the 3.5-inch
  mounts are unused (confirmed 2026-11-12; replaces the review value of two). Its drive-cage step
  shares the CX2151x and CX2177x drive-cage renders (confirmed 2026-11-12).
- Renders: CX2130a and CX2130c supplied unnumbered partial sets (no GPU step, clearance drawings
  or front panel); CX2130x only the exploded view (plus the shared drive-cage renders). Every missing render is a
  grey placeholder. The CX2130b is not built; it has no AIO support (confirmed 2026-11-12).
- Exploded views: the REV20261112 views (supplied 2026-11-12) show the rack ears and pull handles,
  so each part key ends with one "Rack ear & pull handle" callout.
- AIO (CX2130a): the shared Dynatron L34 renders (`illustrations/common/2u-l34-install/`,
  supplied 2026-11-13) and the shared 2U AIO page replace the grey placeholder.
- Clearance drawings (supplied 2026-11-13): each model has its own top view
  (`CX2130{a,c,x}_topAdvanced.webp`); all three share `CX2130_frontAdvanced.webp` (the CX2130x
  folder had none, so it uses the CX2130a/c copy). Drawing values are nominal: 277.3 mm GPU with
  25 mm fans and 67.6 mm CPU cooler, against the 275 mm and 66 mm limits used in the manuals.
- Front I/O: the shared 2U close-up drawings (`2Ua_frontIO.jpg` for the CX2130a, `2Ucx_frontIO.jpg`
  for the CX2130c and CX2130x, supplied 2026-11-13).
