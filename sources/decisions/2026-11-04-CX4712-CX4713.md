## Storage: total mounting locations

Counted as for the other bay models (`2026-11-04-bay-filler-blank-capacity.md`):

| Location | 2.5-inch | 3.5-inch |
|---|---|---|
| Behind the front I/O area | 4 | |
| Rear motherboard tray (two 7 mm or one 15 mm) | 2 | |
| Two 5.25-inch bay filler blanks (four SSDs, or one HDD and two SSDs, each) | 8 | 2 |
| Hot-swap bays behind the magnetic faceplate | | 10 |
| **Total** | **14** | **12** |

Confirmed over the spec review's 6 + 10, which counted neither the blanks nor (for 2.5-inch)
the full blank capacity. Not every location can be used at the same time.

## CX-NAS-HWKIT

A separately packaged kit included with both models, listed on a second hardware-key page
after SBAG-HWKIT and items G–I:

| Letter | Item | Quantity |
|---|---|---|
| J | HDD shoulder screws, 217-735 | 45 |
| K | Thumb screws, 217-737 | 2 |
| L | SAS/SATA (SFF-8482) adapters | 2 |
| M | 5-way SATA power splitters | 2 |
| N | Key pair | 1 |

The thumb screws are illustrated with the 217-736 render; the two differ only in thread
length. The website also states that ten SFF-8482 adapters are preinstalled in the bays and
that no SATA data cables are included.

## Review confirmations

- Hot-swap steps: unlock with the key (N) and slide the retention tray back; after the
  drives are in, slide it forward, secure it with the thumb screws (K) and lock it.
- The fan bracket is secured with green E screws on both models.
- The CX4712 motherboard tray is removable, as on the CX4713; its tray renders will follow.
