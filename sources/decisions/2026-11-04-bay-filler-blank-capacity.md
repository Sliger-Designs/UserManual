# 5.25-inch bay filler blank capacity — November 4, 2026

Confirmed: a 5.25-inch bay filler blank holds either **four 2.5-inch SSDs**, or **one 3.5-inch
HDD and two 2.5-inch SSDs**. Totals count 4 × 2.5-inch and 1 × 3.5-inch mounting locations per
blank. This corrects the website wording ("two 2.5-inch SSD and one 3.5-inch HDD") used in the
earlier storage records.

| Model | Bays | 2.5-inch locations | 3.5-inch locations |
|---|---|---|---|
| CX4150e | 2 | 10 (tray 2 + 4 per blank) | 2 |
| CX4150i | 1 | 6 (tray 2 + blank 4) | 1 |
| CX4170e | 2 | 10 (tray 2 + 4 per blank) | 2 |
| CX4170i | 1 | 14 (tray 2 + cage 8 + blank 4) | 4 (cage 3 + blank 1) |
| CX4200e | 2 | 10 (tray 2 + 4 per blank) | 2 |
| CX4200f | 3 | 14 (tray 2 + 4 per blank) | 3 |
| CX4200i | 1 | 14 (tray 2 + cage 8 + blank 4) | 4 (cage 3 + blank 1) |

Not every location can be used at the same time. The render `drive12.jpg.jpg` (four SSDs on a
blank, supplied with the CX4712/CX4713) is shared as `common/4u-bay-blank/drive12.jpg.jpg` and
shown with the HDD render on each "Mount drives on a filler blank" page.

Supersedes the 2.5-inch totals in `2026-11-04-CX4150e-i-storage-and-cooling.md`,
`2026-11-04-CX4170e-i-storage-and-cooling.md` and `2026-11-04-CX4200-storage-and-cooling.md`.
