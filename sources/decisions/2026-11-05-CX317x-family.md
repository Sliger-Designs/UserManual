# CX3170a / CX3171a / CX3172a — November 5, 2026

The 17-inch-deep 3U chassis with the standard motherboard tray (boards up to 12 × 10.5 inches).
Each is identical to its XL version apart from the tray (`2026-11-05-CX317x-XL-family.md`).

## Tray

Confirmed: the 17-inch non-XL trays are the same parts as the 15-inch trays of the matching
model (CX3150a, CX3151a, CX3152a). Storage follows the 15-inch rules: CX3170a 2 × 2.5-inch
(two 7 mm SSDs or one 15 mm), CX3171a 2 × 2.5-inch (riser tray), CX3172a 2 × 2.5-inch (one under
the ATX PSU, one beside the motherboard). The CX3171a uses the CX3151-GPUKIT.

## Cooling and GPU length

Confirmed: push-pull **is** supported on these three, unlike the 15-inch and XL models: a 360 mm
radiator with six 25 mm fans, maximum GPU length 322 mm (spec review). Other limits (spec
review): 395 mm with no fans, 375 mm with three front fans, 347 mm with a 360 mm radiator and
three fans. Cards 115 mm tall (CX3171a: 150 mm and 72 mm wide on the riser).

## CX3172a

Five full-height slots (website; replaces the catalog's "5x Half Height"); ATX PSU up to 268 mm
including connectors, overlapping ATX and larger boards (five PCIe slots; low-profile USB-header
adapters may be needed).
