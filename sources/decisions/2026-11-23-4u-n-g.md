# CX4280n, CX4250g and CX4280g (4U n and g series) — November 23, 2026

The four 4U n and g models (CX4250n, CX4280n, CX4250g, CX4280g) are now in the private import
files (`sources/original/compatibility-list.csv` and `bulk-create.csv`, private and git-ignored,
updated 2026-11-23). Those files, not the Google Sheet, stay the import source for now. The compatibility
rows only had a few columns filled in. The blank cells were filled from the shared-chassis model
(CX4712 or CX4713) and the website specifications. The bulk-create rows are new, copied from the
CX4712 or CX4713 row with the differences listed below. Facts that neither file holds (outer
dimensions, PSU length, rack-ear screws) are in `products/supplemental.json`. The CX4250n basis is
in `2026-11-20-CX4250n.md`.

The 3U and 4U n and g series share chassis wraps and front panels
(confirmed 2026-11-20).

## CX4280n (28-inch 4U backplane NAS)

- Shares the CX4713 chassis (28-inch). The storage wiring and NAS kit match the
  CX4250n (ten-port backplane, `products/shared/CX-NAS-HWKIT-backplane.json`).
- Own values from the website specifications (`sources/web/cx4280n-specs.txt`, retrieved
  2026-11-20): GPU height 158 mm, GPU length 416 / 393 / 365 / 340 mm, PSU length 268 mm.
- The cover uses the CX4713 product photo and the CX4713 clearance drawings until
  model-specific artwork exists.

## CX4250g (25-inch 4U, eight 5.25-inch bays)

- Shares the CX4712 chassis (25-inch). The ten 3.5-inch hot-swap bays are replaced by
  two banks of four 5.25-inch bays (taken from the Solid Edge assembly), so the CX4712 SSD and
  HDD mounting totals do not apply.
- Storage counts what ships (decided 2026-11-23): the 2U, 3U and 4U g models ship with two
  filler blanks, and buyers of a multi-bay case are expected to fill the other bays with
  5.25-inch devices. Two tray mounts plus two blanks (four 2.5-inch SSDs, or one 3.5-inch HDD
  and two SSDs, each) give 10 × 2.5-inch SSD or 2 × 3.5-inch HDD mounting locations
  (`products/overrides.json`). The same rule applies to the CX4280g and to the 2U and 3U g
  models.
- Own values from the website specifications (`sources/web/cx4250g-specs.txt`, retrieved
  2026-11-20): GPU length 365 / 340 / 312 / 288 mm.

## CX4280g (28-inch 4U, eight 5.25-inch bays)

- Sliger approved this manual on 2026-11-20. The CX4280g has no website page yet and may not
  get one for some time. It is the 28-inch version of the CX4250g, and its manual is nearly
  identical.
- Shares the CX4713 chassis (28-inch), with the same bay change as the CX4250g.
- GPU height, GPU length and PSU length are provisional CX4280n website values (same chassis
  depth and fan wall). Confirm them before release.
- The cover uses the CX4250g product photo (identical from the outside) until a CX4280g photo
  exists.

## Decided 2026-11-23

- n-series storage uses the higher count, as in the product records: 14 × 2.5-inch SSD and
  12 × 3.5-inch HDD mounting locations (bay filler blanks included). The bulk-create rows say so.
- GPU clearance: the CX4280n website gives 416 mm total internal depth and "less than 418mm" on
  the next line. The CX4713 page has the same pair of lines, and the CX4713 manual uses 416 mm
  (approved 2026-11-04). The CX4280n differs from the CX4713 only in the backplane, so its
  manual uses 416 mm too.
