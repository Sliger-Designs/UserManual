# CX3250g, CX3251g and CX3252g (25-inch 3U, six 5.25-inch bays) — November 23, 2026

Website specifications: `sources/web/cx3250g-specs.txt`, `cx3251g-specs.txt`, `cx3252g-specs.txt`
(retrieved 2026-11-20; CX3250g refreshed 2026-11-23 after Sliger removed a 13-inch XL-ATX/EATX
entry that the CX3150a tray does not support).

Decided by Sliger on 2026-11-23:

- **Cooling:** all three have a mid-chassis fan wall made of two parts: a base bracket screwed to
  the chassis floor, and the fan bracket, which lifts out. The fan bracket holds three 120 mm fans
  up to 38 mm thick, or a 120, 240 or 360 mm radiator. Push-pull is not supported, because the
  fans touch the motherboard tray. The optional rear fan adapters are also available. The three
  website pages describe the cooling differently (mid only; front, mid and rear; front and rear),
  and the CX3250g page lists five or six push-pull fans. The guides follow this decision.
- **Trays:** the same motherboard trays as the CX3150a (SFX, eight slots), CX3151a (SFX, riser
  with the CX3151-GPUKIT) and CX3152a (ATX PSU, five slots). The tray pages and tray renders are
  reused, and so are the motherboard, PSU and GPU limits of each tray, except GPU length.
- **Storage:** the tray's two 2.5-inch mounts plus the two included filler blanks give
  10 × 2.5-inch SSD or 2 × 3.5-inch HDD mounting locations (the g rule, `2026-11-23-4u-n-g.md`).
- **Bay banks:** as the 4U g. Remove four 217-499 screws, slide the bank back and lift it out;
  devices fix with 217-477P side screws.

From the website: outer dimensions 635 × 431.8 × 132.59 mm; GPU length 366 mm with no fans,
341 mm with three 25 mm fans, and 313 mm with a 360 mm AIO and three fans. The website's 289 mm
six-fan figure does not apply, because push-pull is not supported. The import rows (git-ignored CSVs) fill the sparse compatibility rows and add
bulk-create rows from the tray models with these values.

Illustrations: each model's own top-view clearance drawing (`TOP_DIMS`, supplied 2026) and website
cover photo. CX3250g: new Solid Edge renders of `CX3250g_INT-MIN` for every chassis, fan-wall, tray
and bay step and the exploded view (cameras framed by Sliger, 2026-11-23). The renders use a "Manual
Orange" highlight style, and real 217-477P bay-device screws are swapped in at render time. Only the
I/O-shield and cable-bar images are still the CX3150a ones. CX3251g and CX3252g will get their own renders (Sliger, 2026-11-23), from `_INT-MIN`
assemblies with their trays and components that Sliger is building. Until then, CX3251g uses
the CX3250g chassis, fan-wall and bay renders, and keeps the CX3151a tray images for comparison. The tray-step renders and front-view clearance drawing of the CX3150a, CX3151a and
CX3152a are reused, and the 15-inch chassis shows around the tray. Grey boxes: the exploded view,
lid, faceplate, tray removal and reinstall, rack ears, all fan-bracket and radiator steps, and the
bay-bank steps.

## Renders for CX3251g and CX3252g (2026-11-24)

Sliger built `CX3251g_INT-MIN` and `CX3252g_INT-MIN` from the current `CX3251g.asm` and `CX3252g.asm`
(the archived pre-2026-11-11 assemblies use older part numbers). Each matches `CX3250g_INT-MIN`: the
`SA-3000g_INT` subfront, the three NF-A12x25 fans with 12 CS-1 fan screws, and the Asetek 360 mm AIO
with 12 × 632 × 30 mm screws, in the same positions, with the model's own `MT3-C1A_INT-MIN` or
`MT3-C2A_INT-MIN` tray. Sliger is not sure which radiator screw ships (the 632 × 30 mm or the
632 × 1.000 inch Asetek kit screw); the renders use the 30 mm one, as on the CX3250g.

The lid, faceplate, fan-wall, tray removal and reinstall, bay, rack-ear, cable-management and exploded
views are rendered from these assemblies with the CX3250g cameras (same chassis). Sliger then added the
motherboard, PSU and GPU screws, the SSDs, the 3D-printed GPU braces (tall `3D-CX3151-006A` and short
`3D-CX3151-005A`, both on the same two tray holes) and the CX3252g PSU screws to the tray assemblies, so the
standoff, I/O-shield, motherboard, PSU, SSD, rear-cover, brace and GPU steps are rendered too. Only CX3251g
9e (slot covers, not modelled) and CX3252g 7b (upright second SSD, not modelled) keep the CX3151a and
CX3152a images.

Confirmed by Sliger on 2026-11-25:

- The CX3251g riser rear cover sits on the outside of the rear panel. Its two screws come out the back,
  the cover slides 10–15 mm toward the PSU, then comes away from the chassis. The CX3251g guide has its
  own rear-cover page for this, viewed from behind.
- Both braces use the same two holes, so the CX3251g brace page has one screw-removal step.
- The I/O-shield opening is shown filled in Manual Orange and the cable-routing channel filled light green
  in the image itself (Sliger's GIMP edits; on the CX3252g the channel continues on the PSU side of the GPU). The green
  overlay polygons are no longer used for these two guides.

## Confirmed later on 2026-11-23

- The 3U fan bracket lifts out with no screws, as on the 4U g. The base bracket under it stays
  screwed to the floor.
- The new g guides use `bay-blank-drives-bank.md`, a copy of the shared filler-blank page that says
  to route cables clear of the fans and radiator, and fit the blank in the bay bank. The released
  guides keep the original page, where "front cooling assembly" is correct.
