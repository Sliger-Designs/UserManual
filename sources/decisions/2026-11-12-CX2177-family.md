# CX2177a, CX2177c and CX2177x (17-inch 2U) — November 12, 2026

The first 2U manuals. Values come from the 2026-11-04 lightning round of the chassis data
review (private workbook, Review Queue), the website specification tabs
(`sources/web/cx2177{a,c,x}-specs.txt`) and the supplied renders.

## Approved values (2026-11-04 lightning round)

- Outer dimensions 431.8 × 431.8 × 87.88 mm (17 × 17 × 3.46 inches), from the website. The
  bulk-create 330.2 mm depth was copied from the CX2130 and is wrong for these models.
- GPU length: 404 mm without front fans; 378 mm with 25 mm fans; 366 mm with 38 mm fans. The
  website's 376 / 363 mm are superseded.
- AIO: the CX2177a takes the four-fan Dynatron L34 (radiator, fans and fan guards 86.5 mm
  thick), leaving 317 mm for the GPU. AIO cooling is not officially supported on the CX2177c
  and CX2177x: they have three fan positions and the three-fan Dynatron L35 interferes with
  their drive brackets.
- Power supply: FlexATX (not ATX), up to 312 mm on the CX2177a and 250 mm on the CX2177c and
  CX2177x (longer units interfere with their drive hardware).
- Storage (total mounting locations; not all usable at once):
  - CX2177a: five 2.5-inch (7 mm) in the rear drive bracket when the 3.5-inch mounts are
    unused; two 3.5-inch up to 20 mm, or one 3.5-inch HDD and one 2.5-inch SSD up to 15 mm.
  - CX2177c: seven 2.5-inch: two at the front drive cage (above the 3.5-inch drives up to
    15 mm, below them 7 mm only) and five 7 mm in the rear drive bracket, usable only when the
    3.5-inch HDD mounts are unused; two 3.5-inch up to 25 mm in the front drive cage; two
    front-accessible 3.5-inch adapter bays for IcyDock or CRU devices.
  - CX2177x: twelve 2.5-inch (seven 7 mm in the front cage and five 7 mm in the rear drive
    bracket when their 3.5-inch mounts are unused, including one above the front 3.5-inch drives
    up to 15 mm and one below them, 7 mm); four 3.5-inch: two up to 25 mm in the front cage, two
    up to 20 mm in the rear drive bracket.
- Motherboards: CX2177a up to XL-ATX/EATX 12.8 × 13 inches and SSI-EEB 12 × 13 inches;
  CX2177c and CX2177x up to SSI-EEB 12 × 13 inches (EATX as 12 × 10.5 inches). On the c and x,
  SSI-CEB and 10.5-inch deep EATX boards are incompatible with 38 mm fans (website).
- Seven half-height PCIe slots, cards up to 72 mm tall; seven slot covers (bulk-create).

## Supplied renders and choices (2026-11-12)

- 2U rack ears are shared by every 2U chassis (`2026-11-04-rackmount-rack-ears.md`); the
  CX2177a/x rack-ear renders show two M4 screws per side, four in total.
- No motherboard tray: boards mount on standoffs in the chassis floor, so the tray pages are
  omitted and 2U versions of the lid, fan, motherboard and front-port pages are used.
- The 2U front panel has a power switch and two USB 3.0 Type-A ports (no USB-C).
- CX2177c: its own, unnumbered renders are used wherever they show the step, even where they
  show an earlier layout. Its front drive cage is a different part from the CX2177x front
  drive bracket, so it has its own pages.
- The supplied 2U exploded views show no rack ears or pull handles; the part keys leave out
  the "Rack ear & pull handle" callout until views with them are supplied.
- Missing renders (AIO, CX2177c clearance drawings and front panel) are grey
  placeholder boxes in the drafts.
- No cable-management renders for any 2U model: there is no motherboard tray to route cables to
  (the 3U and 4U trays have a bend for it). The page stays, text only (2026-11-12).
