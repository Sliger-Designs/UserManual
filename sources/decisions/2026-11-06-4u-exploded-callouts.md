# 4U exploded-view callouts (2026-11-06)

Review of the CX4170a manual, applied to every 4U exploded view (CX4150a/e/i, CX4170a/e/i,
CX4200a/e/f/i):

- The rack ear and pull handle are one callout, "Rack ear & pull handle". The chassis wrap is
  its own callout, "Chassis wrap", instead of "Chassis wrap with rack-ear flanges".
- The subfront and the filter foam get their own callouts. "Faceplate and dust filter" becomes
  "Faceplate".
- Part names follow the 3U part keys: Subfront, Filter foam, Faceplate, Chassis wrap.
- Callouts are numbered from the top of the chassis to the back: lid, subfront, filter foam,
  faceplate, front bracket (bay or fan models), front I/O, rack ear & pull handle,
  chassis wrap, motherboard tray.
- The CX4200a render has no rack ears or pull handles fitted, so that view has no rack-ear
  callout. Its rack-ear installation page is unchanged.
