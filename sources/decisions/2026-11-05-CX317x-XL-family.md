# CX3170a/i XL, CX3171a/i XL, CX3172a/i XL — November 5, 2026

The 17-inch-deep 3U chassis with the XL motherboard tray. Confirmed: the CX3170a and CX3170a XL
are identical except for the tray; the XL tray is deeper (12 × 13-inch SSI-EEB and
12.8 × 13-inch XL-ATX/EATX boards). The same holds for the other XL variants. As on the 15-inch
families, 3170 = SFX PSU, 3171 = riser GPU, 3172 = ATX PSU; "a" = three front fans, "i" = two
front fans and one 5.25-inch bay (the CX3171i XL website's "two 5.25-inch bays" is wrong).

## Model IDs

Repo paths and file names use the website part numbers without spaces (`CX3170aXL`, …); the
manuals show the catalog names (`CX3170a XL`, `display_name`). `scripts/manual_paths.py` maps
each ID to its catalog SKU and render folder ("CX3170a XL") and website slug ("cx3170a_xl").

## Storage (same tray rules as the 15-inch families)

Only the CX3170a XL and CX3171a XL trays carry the drive cage (eight 2.5-inch or three 3.5-inch
drives, or mixed layouts; it can be fitted in two positions).

| Model | 2.5-inch locations | 3.5-inch locations |
|---|---|---|
| CX3170a XL, CX3171a XL | 10 (tray 2 + drive cage 8) | 3 (drive cage) |
| CX3170i XL, CX3171i XL | 9 (tray 2 + filler blank 4 + bay bracket top 2 + under the bay bracket 1) | 2 |
| CX3172a XL | 2 (one under the PSU, one beside the motherboard) | 0 |
| CX3172i XL | 9 (ATX tray 2 + filler blank 4 + bay bracket 3) | 2 |

## Cooling and GPU length

Approved (spec review): 395 mm with no fans, 375 mm with front fans, 347 mm with a radiator and
fans; cards 115 mm tall (150 mm and 72 mm wide on the riser models). Push-pull is not officially
supported on any XL model: the spec review's 322 mm "max cooling" value is not published, and
347 mm is the cooling maximum. "a" models take one 360 mm, one 240 mm or two 120 mm radiators
(three fans); "i" models one 240 mm or one 120 mm radiator up to 277 mm (two fans), with the
countersunk-screw note for the 5.25-inch device screws.

## Riser models

The CX3171a XL and CX3171i XL ship the CX3151-GPUKIT. The drawings show three brace positions,
set by the motherboard's mounting holes; the brace is not compatible with the furthest-back
corner standoff of 13-inch EATX/SSI-EEB boards.

## ATX-PSU models

Five full-height slots (website; replaces the catalog's "5x Half Height"); ATX PSU up to 268 mm
including connectors. The PSU overlaps ATX and larger boards, limiting them to five PCIe slots,
and some USB headers may need low-profile adapters.
