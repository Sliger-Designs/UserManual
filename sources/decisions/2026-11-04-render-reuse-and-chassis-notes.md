# Render reuse and chassis notes — November 4, 2026

- Renders can be reused across models when they show the same part and view (for example,
  the CX3170a XL lid render for the CX3171a). Reuse is noted in the inventory and listed in
  the manual's pull request for review.
- 2U chassis never have a motherboard tray. 2U AIO renders may be supplied in the future.
- The CX3701 and CX3702 do not use motherboard standoffs. Their successors, currently website
  concepts, differ: the CX3150n (CX3701 "v2") uses no standoffs; the CX3180n (CX3702 "v2")
  uses standoffs.
- The CX3151i I/O-shield step can use the CX3151a or CX3151x render.

Outstanding artwork is tracked in `illustrations/ARTWORK-REQUESTS.md`.
