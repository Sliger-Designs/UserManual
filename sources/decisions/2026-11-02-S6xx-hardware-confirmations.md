# S630 / S640 hardware follow-up — November 2, 2026

Additional source renders were supplied under `illustrations/common/hardware/`
and `illustrations/common/S6xx-hwkit/` while finishing both manuals.

The new screw renders replace only the low-resolution top and side detail views.
Retain the existing colored screw illustrations in the hardware key. The new slot
cover render replaces the earlier slot-cover reference image.

## Artwork matching

Yellow A uses the 217-499 top/side renders; green D uses 217-019; blue E uses
217-477P. Existing colored illustrations stay in all three entries. D's earlier
"hex screws" label is corrected to "flat-head screws": both the existing photos
and the new render show the countersunk head. The initial .187-inch / 217-018A
selection was subsequently corrected to .250-inch / 217-019.
The new 4-40 × .250-inch render is not substituted for the previously confirmed
M3.5 × 5 mm red B screw. The 217-621 top/side renders were subsequently supplied
and identified as B's replacement artwork. Their original filenames
say `M3 X 5mm`; this does not override the confirmed M3.5 × 5 mm text.

## Blue E screws

MRP lists 6 mm, CAD labels the screw 8 mm, and the physical
screw has 5 mm of thread and measures 8 mm overall. Use the physical measurements
explicitly; do not present the CAD overall dimension as the threaded length.
The manual identifies E as an M3 screw with 5 mm of thread, 8 mm overall, replacing
the earlier draft M3 × 6 mm label. The supplied 217-477P top/side renders illustrate
this item. Original filenames, MRP/CAD evidence and supplied images stay unchanged.

## Standoff C

The new 217-734 filename lists .150-inch male thread and a .250-inch female head.
The earlier confirmed manual specification is .125-inch male thread and
.250-inch length. The new render was initially confirmed to show a different
part. In follow-up, that render was explicitly approved as suitable artwork
for both manuals. Replace C's top/side photos with 217-734 while keeping the actual
.125-inch male-thread specification and existing colored illustration. The .150-inch
filename describes the illustrated reference part, not a revised kit specification.

## Green screw correction — SFF now, rackmount follow-up

Green S630/S640 D is specified as **6-32 × .250-inch, part 217-019**.
Use its top and side renders only; retain the existing green-marked illustration,
quantity, and mounting applications. This supersedes the earlier .187-inch value.

This correction explicitly also applies to rackmount cases when
those manuals are updated later. The green screw is **E** in `SBAG-HWKIT`, not D
(rackmount D is blue). During that update, use the same 217-019 specification and
top/side renders while retaining the green illustration and rackmount quantities.
Rackmount manuals and their shared kit record have not been changed in this SFF pass.
