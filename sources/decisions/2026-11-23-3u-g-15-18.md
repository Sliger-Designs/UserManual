# CX3150g and CX3180g (15- and 18-inch 3U, six 5.25-inch bays) — November 23, 2026

Sliger asked for these two guides on 2026-11-23. Neither model has a website page yet (both
returned 404 on 2026-11-23) or a catalog row, so these facts come from Sliger and from the shared
chassis:

- Same chassis and internals as the CX3701 / CX3150n (15-inch, Mini-ITX) and the CX3702 / CX3180n
  (18-inch, MicroATX): motherboard tray, side and rear fans, SFX PSU, motherboard support and GPU
  limits. The CX3180g, like the CX3180n, has 261 mm GPU clearance and no AIO support.
- The front has six 5.25-inch bays in two columns of three instead of the ten 3.5-inch NAS bays,
  with the same front I/O (power switch, two USB-A, one USB-C).
- There are no other 2.5-inch mounts (the four SSD mounts behind the front I/O are not present).
  Storage counts what ships (the g rule, `2026-11-23-4u-n-g.md`): two included filler blanks give
  8 × 2.5-inch SSD or 2 × 3.5-inch HDD mounting locations.
- A bay bank comes out as on the 4U g models: remove the four 217-499 bank-to-subfront screws,
  slide the bank back, and lift it out; devices fix with 217-477P side screws.

Import rows (git-ignored CSVs) are copies of the CX3150n and CX3180n rows with the front bays and
storage changed. Supplemental and override facts are copied from those models, with the storage
decision above.

Illustrations: new Solid Edge renders of `CX3150g.asm` and `CX3180g.asm` (cameras framed by Sliger,
2026-11-23) for the lid, faceplate, empty tray out and in, the side and rear fan brackets, rack ears and
the exploded view. Sliger added the fan brackets' screws and the missing tray screws to both models
first (four 217-018A on the tray, four on the side bracket; the rear bracket uses four 217-499). The
bay-bank steps use the CX3250g renders (same SA-3000g subfront; framed without the tray). The
fan-on-bracket close-ups and the motherboard, PSU and GPU steps keep the CX3701 and CX3702 images,
because these assemblies have no fans or components. Grey box: the top-view clearance drawing. The
covers use the CX3701 and CX3702 product photos (identical from the outside).

## Renders from the _INT-MIN models (2026-11-25)

Sliger built `CX3150g _INT-MIN` and `CX3180g_INT-MIN` with populated trays. The tray, fan-bracket, PSU, GPU, bay-bank and
tray out/in steps are rendered from them (`scripts/solidedge/make_specs_3u_g15_int.py`):

- The CX3180g (and CX3180n) has a new MT3-U0A tray with eight 217-734 standoffs, so its guide uses the standoff pages and
  says the board mounts on the included standoffs.
- The side fan bracket, its fan and fan screws are screwed to the chassis wrap, not the tray; they stay put when the tray
  moves. Tray steps highlight the tray, the rear fan bracket and its screws (and the CX3180g PSU bracket); components keep
  their own look.
- Tray removal comes before the fan pages, so it shows no fans; the reinstall shows the populated tray.
