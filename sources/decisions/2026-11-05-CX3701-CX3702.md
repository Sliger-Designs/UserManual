# CX3701 / CX3702 — November 5, 2026

3U NAS chassis with ten 3.5-inch hot-swap bays behind a magnetic faceplate: CX3701 (15 inches
deep, Mini-ITX) and CX3702 (18 inches deep, Mini-ITX, Deep Mini-ITX and MicroATX).

## Confirmed

- **No motherboard standoffs** on either model (2026-11-04): the board mounts directly to the
  tray. Shared pages `motherboard-preparation-no-standoffs.md` and
  `motherboard-installation-no-standoffs.md`.
- **Hardware:** both ship the CX-NAS-HWKIT, as the CX4712/CX4713 (shoulder screws, thumb screws,
  spare SAS/SATA adapters, SATA power splitters, key pair).
- **Front ports:** the shared 3U "a" front drawing (same front I/O module; no NAS front render).
- **CX3701 liquid cooling:** one 92 mm AIO (Asetek 645LT) on a 3D-printed 120-to-92 mm adapter,
  not officially supported (spec review). Text-only AIO page; renders requested.
- **CX3702 HDD lock step:** reuses `8e_CX3701_LOCKED HDD.jpg` (same retention tray and lock).

## Values (spec review and website)

| | CX3701 | CX3702 |
|---|---|---|
| Outer dimensions | 381 × 431.8 × 132.59 mm | 457.2 × 431.8 × 132.59 mm |
| PCIe slots | 2 full-height | 4 full-height |
| GPU length / height | 182 mm / 115 mm | 245 mm / 115 mm |
| Liquid cooling | 92 mm AIO on adapter (not officially supported) | 120 mm AIO (Asetek 650LKS, up to 27 mm thick), or 92 mm on adapter |
| Storage | 4 × 2.5-inch (up to 7 mm, behind the front I/O), 10 × 3.5-inch hot-swap | same |

Two 120 mm fan positions (side vent and rear), up to 38 mm thick. With a dual-slot card the side
vent fan must be a slim 15 mm fan (CX3701) or may need to be (CX3702), per the website.
