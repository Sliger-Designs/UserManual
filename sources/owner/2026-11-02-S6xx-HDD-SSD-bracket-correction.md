# S6xx HDD/SSD bracket illustration correction

Owner clarification on November 2, 2026, during the S630 page 19 review:

- The upper bracket illustration shows one 3.5-inch HDD with a 2.5-inch SSD
  mounted underneath it. The blue E screws secure the SSD, not bracket joints.
- The lower illustration shows two 3.5-inch HDDs in the stacked bracket.

The shared procedure and both model captions are corrected because S630 and
S640 use the same bracket illustrations. Hardware-key references to E screws
fastening storage-bracket joints are removed.

The owner intends to complete one more S630 read-through before publication and
reset its revision history to v1.0 at release. This correction does not mark the
manual released or reset its current review revision.
