Storage
Four (4) 2.5" drives up to 7mm thick
Four(4) 2.5" SSD/HDD mounting points (behind power button and USB front panel area)
The backplane has two SATA ports on the edge closest to these four SSD mounting locations allowing the user to use short ~6" SATA cables to go between the SSD and backplane. Data for these two ports is fed through the SFF-8643 port to the HBA/Motherboard
3.5" Bays
Ten (10) 3.5" drives
Ten(10) 3.5" SAS or SATA Hard Drive Disks accessible by removing the magnetic Faceplate
Case includes one(1) ten(10) port SATA/SAS PCB backplane (preinstalled in case) with SFF-8643 Data plugs
Case has a locking mechanism and key, as well as thumb screws, to secure drives
Case also includes three(3) power cables for the backplane; one(1) dual 4-pin board side connector to one(1) EPS PSU side plug and two(2) single 4-pin board side connector to one 6-pin PCIe PSU side plug
Case does
NOT
include any data cables
Four(4) shoulder screws attach to each 3.5" HDD
The shoulder screws guide HDDs through slots and align the drive to the SATA/SAS plug on the backplane
User must connect SFF-8643 Board-side data and power cables on inside of case to the backplane
Hot-swap support depends on HBA / RAID card, motherboard and OS
A SAS controller can control SATA drives, but a SATA controller cannot control SAS drives <
