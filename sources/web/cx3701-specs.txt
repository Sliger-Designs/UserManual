Storage
Four (4) 2.5" drives up to 7mm thick
Four(4) 2.5" SSD/HDD mounting points (behind power button and USB front panel area)
Ten (10) 3.5" drives
Ten(10) 3.5" SAS or SATA Hard Drive Disks accessible by removing the magnetic Faceplate
Case includes ten(10) SATA/SAS SFF 8482 male to SATA female adapters (preinstalled in case)
Case has a locking mechanism and key, as well as thumb screws, to secure drives
Case also includes two(2) SATA power distribution cables (One(1) SATA power male to five(5) SATA power female)
Case does NOT include any data cables
Four(4) shoulder screws attach to each 3.5" HDD
The shoulder screws guide HDDs through slots and align the drive to a backplane mounted SATA/SAS to SATA adapter
User must connect SATA data and power cables on inside of case to these SATA/SAS to SATA adapters
Hot-swap support depends on HBA / RAID card, motherboard and OS
Please note that connecting a SAS drive to a SATA port will operate as a SATA drive
A SAS controller can control SATA drives, but a SATA controller cannot control SAS drives
