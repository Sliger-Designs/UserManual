### 1. Seat the card in the riser

Remove the slot covers for the card's width. Lay the GPU flat over the motherboard and press its edge connector fully into the riser slot. Fit {{ hw('G') }} over unused openings and secure them with {{ hw('A') }}.

### 2. Refit the support bracket

Return the GPU support bracket against the card's rear bracket and secure it with the {{ hw('A') }}.

<p class="info-note" role="note">Cards up to <strong>{{ facts.gpu_height_max_mm }} mm</strong> tall including power connectors and up to 2.5 slots (50 mm) wide. An internal support bracket is available for select GPU models on request.</p>
