The two front-accessible 3.5-inch adapter bays take IcyDock or CRU storage devices. Filler blanks are available as add-ons; each mounts one 2.5-inch SSD or one HDD up to 15 mm thick.

### 1. Release the bay bracket

Remove the screw beneath the front of the bay bracket and slide the bracket out.

### 2. Fit the devices

Install the adapter devices or filler blanks in the bracket, slide it back in, and refit the screw.
