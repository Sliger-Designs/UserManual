### 1. Lower the motherboard

Place the motherboard onto the {{ hw("C") }}. Align its rear ports with the I/O opening and its mounting holes with the standoffs.

### 2. Secure the board

Install {{ hw("A") }} through the board and into the standoffs.

Tighten by hand to avoid damaging the board or loosening the standoffs during future removal.

Follow your motherboard's instructions for CPU, memory, M.2 drives, and cable connections.
