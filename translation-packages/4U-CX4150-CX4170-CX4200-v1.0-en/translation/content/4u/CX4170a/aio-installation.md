### 1. Position the fans

Place three 120 mm fans between the subfront and the 360 mm radiator. Remove any fan screws occupying the radiator mounting positions.

### 2. Align and secure the assembly

Align all twelve mounting points in the panel, fans, and radiator. Use the cooler manufacturer's specified radiator screws through the panel and fan frames into the radiator.

Incorrect screw length can damage the radiator. Check that the radiator clears the front I/O assembly before tightening.
