### Attach the PSU bracket

S640 supports ATX, SFX and SFX-L power supplies. Select the bracket matching the PSU form factor.

1. Attach the bracket to the PSU with yellow-marked 6-32 screws **A**.
2. Choose a position compatible with the motherboard and other components.
3. Secure the bracket to the chassis with four green-marked 6-32 screws **D**.
4. Check cable reach, connector space and the PSU intake/exhaust path.

<p class="info-note" role="note">An ATX PSU at the rear limits the motherboard to ATX. For EATX, place the PSU at the front or on the side fan bracket. The views show alternative assemblies, not simultaneous PSU installations.</p>
