### Fit two 3.5-inch HDDs to the stacked bracket

The illustration shows two 3.5-inch HDDs in the stacked bracket assembly. Align each drive with the side-rail holes and secure it with yellow-marked 6-32 screws **A**. Tighten by hand without overtightening.

Mount the S630-prefix bracket at a suitable fan mounting location using green-marked screws **D**. Check clearance to cooling and other components. Connect the drives without straining the cables and verify the panels close freely.
