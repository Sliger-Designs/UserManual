### Route cables and check the assembly

- Keep power and data cables clear of fans, panel retainers, and sharp edges.
- Check the motherboard power, CPU power, GPU power, front I/O, and drive connections against the component manuals.
- Confirm that no loose fasteners or unused standoffs remain inside the case.
- Inspect the PSU intake and exhaust paths for obstruction.
- Check bracket retention and component clearances before replacing the panels.

### Refit the panels

Align the real panel retainers and press near their attachment points. Do not force a panel against a cable or component.

If the case is used in a flipped orientation, verify the front I/O location, cable slack, feet, ventilation, and panel retention in that configuration before powering on.

### Initial power-on

Reconnect the required external cables and power. Check fan rotation and normal operation before sustained use. If anything contacts a fan or a panel cannot seat, shut down and correct the interference.
