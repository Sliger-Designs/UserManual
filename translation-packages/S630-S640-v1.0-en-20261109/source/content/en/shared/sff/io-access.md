### Move the front I/O to the other side

1. Power down and disconnect external cables. Remove the top panel.
2. Remove the two green-marked screws **D** from above the I/O assembly.
3. Lift the assembly straight up without pulling on its cables.
4. Remove the bottom panel and fit the assembly on the opposite side of the case.
5. Secure it with two green-marked screws **D**. Route the cables without pinching or tension, then reinstall the panels.

Both models have one USB-A port, one USB-C port and one power/status switch. The supplied image shows the removal direction.
