### Fit SSDs to the two-drive bracket

Secure the two SSDs to the bracket with blue-marked M3 screws **E**, keeping the connectors accessible.

The p-5-100013b bracket attaches at fan mounting locations using red-marked screws **B** or blue-marked screws **E**. Select a position with clearance to the PSU, GPU, cooling, SATA connectors and cables.

Storage and cooling share mounting space. Select the bracket combination around the actual PSU, GPU, fans, radiator and cable paths.
