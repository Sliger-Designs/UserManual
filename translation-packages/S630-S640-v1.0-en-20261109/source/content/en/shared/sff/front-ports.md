Use your motherboard manual to identify matching headers and pin assignments. The numbered front-panel locations correspond to the connector drawings and legend.

### 1. Power switch & status light

Momentary power switch with status light. Connect switch and LED leads to the front-panel header; observe the motherboard's LED polarity markings.

### 2. USB Type-A port

One USB 3.0 Type-A port. Connect its keyed internal cable to the matching motherboard USB header.

### 3. USB Type-C port

One USB 3.2 Type-C port. Connect the internal Type-E cable to a compatible motherboard header.
