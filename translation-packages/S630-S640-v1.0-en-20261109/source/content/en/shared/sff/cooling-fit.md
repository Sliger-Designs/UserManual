### Fit fans and a radiator

Choose a mounting location compatible with the motherboard, PSU, GPU and storage: up to 360 mm at the top, bottom or side; 240 mm at the front; 120 mm at the rear.

The side fan bracket is secured by four green-marked screws **D**. Remove them to release the bracket, fit the fans/radiator using the component-specified screws, then refit the bracket with four **D** screws.

<p class="info-note" role="note">Check stack thickness, tubing, cable access and panel closure. Mounting options depend on the installed components; the views do not promise simultaneous fit at every location.</p>
