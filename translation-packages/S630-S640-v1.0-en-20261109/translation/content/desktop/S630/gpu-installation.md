### Install the card

1. Loosen or remove the two hex-head screws retaining the PCIe dust cover and remove the necessary slot covers.
2. Select the motherboard slot, open its latch if fitted, and align the card with the slot and rear opening. Seat it evenly until the latch engages, where applicable.
3. Secure the card's mounting bracket to the PCIe retention flange using the blue-marked M3 screws **E**.
4. Fit slot covers **F** over unused openings and secure them with screws **E**. Refit the dust cover and its retaining screws.

<p class="info-note" role="note">Check power-connector and cable-bend clearance with the chosen cooling, PSU and storage configuration.</p>
