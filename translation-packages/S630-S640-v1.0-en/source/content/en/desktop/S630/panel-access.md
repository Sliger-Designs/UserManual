### Remove the exterior panels

The side, top and bottom panels use ball studs. Pull near a panel edge to release it, then set it aside.

Release one edge of the front panel, then the opposite edge, keeping its push pins aligned to avoid bending them.

The side panels can swap sides, and the front panel can be installed with either end at the top.

### Choose the I/O position

The illustration shows the single power/USB assembly in two alternative positions: **1** for the standard arrangement or **2** for the opposite-side arrangement. Only one assembly is installed. Fit the front panel to match the chosen position and follow the relocation procedure on the next page.
