### Attach the PSU bracket

S630 supports ATX, SFX and SFX-L power supplies. Select the bracket matching the PSU form factor.

1. Attach the bracket to the PSU with yellow-marked 6-32 screws **A**.
2. Choose a mounting position that clears the motherboard, GPU, cooling and storage.
3. Secure the bracket to the chassis with four green-marked 6-32 screws **D**.
4. Check cable reach, connector space and the PSU intake/exhaust path before tightening.
