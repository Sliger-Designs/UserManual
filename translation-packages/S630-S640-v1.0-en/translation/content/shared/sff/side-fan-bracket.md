### Remove and refit the bracket

Each side fan bracket attaches to the chassis with four green-marked 6-32 × 0.25-inch screws **D**.

1. Remove the four **D** screws and lift the bracket away from the chassis to make room for component installation.
2. Fit fans or a radiator to the removed bracket using the component-specified screws, as described on the following page.
3. Align the bracket with its chassis mounting holes and secure it with four **D** screws. Tighten by hand and check clearance to the motherboard, cables and installed components.

{% if model == 'S630' %}
<p class="info-note" role="note">S630 supports one side fan bracket, installed in either of two mounting positions. Use four <strong>D</strong> screws to secure the bracket in the chosen position.</p>
{% else %}
<p class="info-note" role="note">S640 supports a second side fan bracket, purchased separately. Use four <strong>D</strong> screws for each bracket.</p>
