### Fit one 3.5-inch HDD and one 2.5-inch SSD

The illustration shows one 3.5-inch HDD with a 2.5-inch SSD mounted beneath it in the same bracket assembly. Secure the HDD to the side rails with yellow-marked 6-32 screws **A**. Secure the SSD beneath the HDD with blue-marked M3 screws **E**.

Mount the S630-prefix bracket at a suitable fan mounting location with green-marked screws **D**. Check space for the SATA connectors and cable bends, and follow the drive manufacturer's screw-length limits.
