### Fit SSDs to the four-drive bracket

Align the four SSDs with the bracket holes and secure them with blue-marked M3 screws **E** as shown. Keep the SATA connectors accessible and follow the drive manufacturer's screw-length limits.

S630-prefix storage brackets attach at fan mounting locations using green-marked screws **D**. Choose a location that clears the other components and allows cable access; a drive bracket occupies mounting space that may otherwise be used for cooling.
