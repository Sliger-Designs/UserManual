### 1. Attach the second fan bank

A 360 mm radiator may use three 120 mm fans on each side, six in total. Align the additional fans with the rear of the radiator and secure them using the radiator manufacturer's specified screws.

### 2. Mount the complete assembly

Align the front fan bank, radiator, and mounting panel. Install the specified screws through the panel and front fans into the radiator.
