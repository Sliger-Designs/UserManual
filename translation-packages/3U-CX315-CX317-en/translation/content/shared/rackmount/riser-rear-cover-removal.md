The rear cover closes the three horizontal PCIe slots above the riser. Remove it before installing the GPU.

### 1. Remove the cover screws

Remove the two {{ hw('A') }} securing the rear cover to the chassis.

### 2. Release the cover

Pull the cover away from the rear panel.

### 3. Lift the cover out

Lift the cover clear of the slot openings and set it aside with its screws for reassembly.
