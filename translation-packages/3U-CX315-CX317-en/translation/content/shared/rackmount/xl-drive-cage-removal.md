The XL motherboard tray carries a removable drive cage. Removing it makes cooling and motherboard installation easier.

### 1. Remove the side screw

Remove the {{ hw('E') }} securing the drive cage through the side of the chassis.

### 2. Remove the cage screws

Remove the two {{ hw('A') }} securing the cage to the tray.

### 3. Lift the cage out

Lift the cage up and out of the chassis. Disconnect drive cables first if drives are already installed.
