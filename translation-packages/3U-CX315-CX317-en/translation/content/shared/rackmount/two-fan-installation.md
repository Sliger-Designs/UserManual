### 1. Align the fans

Align two 120 mm fans with the front mounting panel. Each fan uses four mounting points, eight in total.

### 2. Secure the fans

Use the self-tapping fan screws supplied with your fans. Pass them through the mounting panel and into the fan frames.

Tighten sufficiently to prevent movement and vibration.
