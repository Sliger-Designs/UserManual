### 1. Route and secure cables

Run front-panel, power, PCIe, and fan cables along the front edge of the motherboard tray's cable-management bar, highlighted in green.

Secure cables with ties and bundle excess cable behind the PSU. Keep cables clear of fan blades and tray engagement points.

Release any ties that anchor cables to the fixed chassis before removing the tray again.

### 2. Refit the filter and faceplate

Position the filter and align the faceplate's locating pins. Press gently to seat both sides.

### 3. Refit the lid

Align the lid flanges with the chassis slots, lower the lid, and slide it forward. Reinstall its two rear {{ hw("A") }}.
