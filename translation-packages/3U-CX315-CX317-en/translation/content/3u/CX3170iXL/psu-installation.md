### 1. Position the PSU

Place your SFX or SFX-L power supply into its mounting location on the tray.

Most PSUs mount with the fan toward the chassis wall. The fan may instead face the motherboard depending on the PSU model. Check the PSU manufacturer's instructions and the available intake clearance.

### 2. Secure the PSU

Install {{ hw("A") }} through the tray's rear panel into the power supply. Tighten by hand.

Route the PSU cables so they do not obstruct the tray's engagement points.
