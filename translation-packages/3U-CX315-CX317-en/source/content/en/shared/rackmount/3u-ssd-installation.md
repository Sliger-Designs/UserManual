### 1. Position the SSD

The motherboard tray has two pairs of 2.5-inch mounting holes between the power supply and the motherboard I/O shield. Install either two SSDs up to 7 mm thick, or one SSD up to 15 mm thick.

Position each drive with its connectors facing upward and its underside facing the power supply.

### 2. Secure the drives

Secure each drive with two {{ hw('B') }} through the tray. Two 7 mm drives use four screws in total. Tighten by hand without forcing the threads.

<p class="info-note" role="note">For M.2 storage, follow the motherboard's installation instructions.</p>
