To the best of our knowledge, all computer chassis manufactured by Sliger Designs, LLC adhere to ROHS, REACH, and Conflict Minerals regulations. All Sliger products are produced in the United States of America and primarily utilize materials sourced domestically.

### ROHS STATEMENT

This product is compliant to implemented RoHS (Restriction of Hazardous Substances) type regulations worldwide, including but not limited to China, India, Japan, Korea and European Union.

### REACH STATEMENT

REACH (Registration, Evaluation, Authorization and Restriction of Chemicals, EC 1907/2006) is the European Union's (EU) chemical substances regulatory framework. REACH requires Sliger Designs, LLC to provide customers with sufficient information on Substances of Very High Concern (SVHC) contained in products in concentration above 0.1% weight by weight (w/w) to allow safe use of the product.

Certificates of Compliance are available upon request.
