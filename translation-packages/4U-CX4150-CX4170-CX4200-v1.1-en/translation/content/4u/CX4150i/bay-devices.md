The bay holds either the filler blank or a 5.25-inch device. The filler blank holds four 2.5-inch SSDs, or one 3.5-inch HDD and up to two 2.5-inch SSDs.

### 1. Remove the filler blank

To fit a 5.25-inch device, slide the filler blank out of the bracket.

### 2. Install the 5.25-inch device

Slide the device into the bracket. Align its mounting holes with the bracket and secure it with {{ hw('D') }} on both sides. A 5.25-inch to 3.5-inch device adapter is available separately.

### 3. Or keep the filler blank

Without a 5.25-inch device, refit the filler blank to the bracket with {{ hw('A') }}. To mount drives on the blank, see {{ ref('bay-blank-drives', 'Mount drives on a filler blank') }}.
