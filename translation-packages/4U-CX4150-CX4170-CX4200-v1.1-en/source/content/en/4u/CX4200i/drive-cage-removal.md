### 1. Remove the retaining screw

Remove the one {{ hw('E') }} securing the internal drive cage at the side of the chassis.

### 2. Lift the cage out

Slide the cage upward and lift it free of the chassis. Disconnect drive cables first if drives are already installed.

### Check the selected cooling layout

The cage occupies space beside the front cooling assembly. Six-fan push-pull is incompatible with the cage. Review {{ ref('cooling-clearance','Radiator and drive-cage clearance') }} before selecting the final drive arrangement.
