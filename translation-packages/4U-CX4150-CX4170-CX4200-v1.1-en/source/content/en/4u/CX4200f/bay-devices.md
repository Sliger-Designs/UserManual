Each bay holds a filler blank or a 5.25-inch device. Each blank holds four 2.5-inch SSDs, or one 3.5-inch HDD and up to two 2.5-inch SSDs.

### 1. Remove the filler blanks

For each bay that will hold a device, remove the {{ hw('A') }} securing its blank, then slide the blank out of the front of the bracket.

### 2. Install the 5.25-inch devices

Slide each device in from the front and secure it on both sides with screws that match its thread: {{ hw('D') }} or {{ hw('B') }} for M3, {{ hw('A') }} for 6-32.

<p class="info-note" role="note"><strong>With a 240 mm AIO:</strong> the radiator comes close to these screws. Countersunk screws are recommended: {{ hw('B') }} for M3, {{ hw('E') }} for 6-32.</p>

### 3. Keep the blanks in unused bays

Leave a blank, secured with its {{ hw('A') }}, in any bay without a device. To mount drives on a blank, see {{ ref('bay-blank-drives', 'Mount drives on a filler blank') }}. A 5.25-inch to 3.5-inch device adapter is available separately.
