Removing the 5.25-inch bay bracket gives access to its filler blanks and makes cooling and motherboard installation easier.

### 1. Remove the bracket screws

Remove the {{ hw('A') }} at the front and the {{ hw('E') }} at the side of the chassis that secure the bay bracket.

### 2. Lift the bracket out

Lift the bracket straight up and out of the chassis. Disconnect drive cables first if drives are already installed.
