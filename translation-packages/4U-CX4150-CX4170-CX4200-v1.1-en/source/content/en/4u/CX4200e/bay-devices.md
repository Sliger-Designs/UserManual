Each bay holds either a filler blank or a 5.25-inch device. Each filler blank holds four 2.5-inch SSDs, or one 3.5-inch HDD and up to two 2.5-inch SSDs.

### 1. Remove a filler blank

To use a bay for a 5.25-inch device, remove the {{ hw('A') }} securing its filler blank, then slide the blank out of the front of the bracket.

### 2. Install the 5.25-inch device

Slide the device into the bracket from the front and secure it with {{ hw('D') }} on both sides. A 5.25-inch to 3.5-inch device adapter is available separately.

<p class="info-note" role="note"><strong>With a 240 mm AIO:</strong> the radiator can touch a hex-head screw retaining a 5.25-inch device. Use a countersunk (flat-head) screw there instead: {{ hw('B') }} for M3-threaded devices, or {{ hw('E') }} for 6-32-threaded devices.</p>

### 3. Keep the blanks in unused bays

Leave the filler blank fitted in any bay without a device, secured with its {{ hw('A') }}. To mount drives on a blank, see {{ ref('bay-blank-drives', 'Mount drives on a filler blank') }}.
