### 1. Check the configuration first

A compatible 360 mm radiator may use three 120 mm fans on each side. **This six-fan arrangement conflicts with the internal drive cage.** Remove the cage before assembling this configuration.

### 2. Attach the second fan bank

Align the three additional fans with the rear of the radiator. Secure them using the radiator manufacturer's specified screws.

### 3. Mount the complete assembly

Align the front fan bank, radiator, and subfront. Install the specified screws through the subfront and front fans into the radiator.

The assembly must clear the motherboard tray, front I/O, and all installed components. With the specified Asetek radiator and six 25 mm fans, maximum GPU length is **{{ facts.gpu_length_max_cooling_mm }} mm**.
