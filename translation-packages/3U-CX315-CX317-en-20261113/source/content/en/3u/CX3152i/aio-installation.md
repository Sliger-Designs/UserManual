### 1. Position the fans

Place two 120 mm fans between the subfront and the 240 mm radiator. Remove any fan screws occupying the radiator mounting positions.

### 2. Align and secure the assembly

Align all eight mounting points in the subfront, fans, and radiator. Use the cooler manufacturer's radiator screws through the subfront and fans into the radiator.

Incorrect screw length can damage the radiator. Check that the radiator clears the front I/O assembly and the 5.25-inch bay bracket before tightening.

### Check component clearance

With the specified Asetek 240 mm AIO and two 25 mm fans, maximum GPU length is **{{ facts.gpu_length_fans_aio_mm }} mm**. A single 120 mm radiator mounts in the same position. Radiators up to 277 mm long fit.

<p class="info-note" role="note"><strong>5.25-inch device screws:</strong> a 240 mm radiator can touch a hex-head screw retaining a device in the bay. Use a countersunk screw in that position, {{ hw('B') }} or {{ hw('E') }} to match the device's thread, or leave it out; see {{ ref('bay-devices', 'Fit 5.25-inch devices or the filler blank') }}.</p>

<p class="info-note" role="note">Four-fan push-pull is not officially supported.</p>
