<p class="info-note" role="note">The drive cage holds <strong>eight 2.5-inch SSDs</strong>, <strong>two 3.5-inch HDDs and three SSDs</strong>, <strong>one HDD and five SSDs</strong>, or <strong>three HDDs</strong>. Do not add capacities from different layouts.</p>

### 1. Mount 3.5-inch HDDs

Align each HDD's mounting holes with the holes in the top and bottom of the cage and secure it with {{ hw('A') }}.

### 2. Mount 2.5-inch SSDs

Align each SSD with the holes in the cage and secure it with {{ hw('D') }}.

Position drives so their connectors can be reached after the cage is installed.
