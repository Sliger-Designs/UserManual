### 1. Position the ATX power supply

Lower the ATX PSU into its mounting location on the motherboard tray, above the edge of the motherboard. Install the SSD under the PSU first; see {{ ref('ssd-installation', 'Install 2.5-inch SSDs') }}.

### 2. Secure the PSU

Install {{ hw('A') }} through the tray's rear panel into the PSU. Tighten by hand and route cables clear of tray engagement points.

<p class="info-note" role="note">Maximum PSU length is <strong>{{ facts.psu_length_max_mm }} mm including connectors</strong>. The PSU overlaps the edge of SSI-CEB, EATX and ATX motherboards, which limits them to five PCIe slots, and leaves 19.6 mm above the board: some USB headers may need low-profile adapters.</p>
