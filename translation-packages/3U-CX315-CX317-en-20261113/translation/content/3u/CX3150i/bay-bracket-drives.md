The bay bracket has further drive positions: two 2.5-inch SSDs or one 3.5-inch HDD on its top, and one 2.5-inch SSD underneath.

### 1. Mount two SSDs on top

Align the SSDs with the mounting holes in the top of the bracket and secure each drive with {{ hw('D') }} through the bracket.

### 2. Or mount a 3.5-inch HDD on top

Align the HDD with the mounting holes in the top of the bracket and secure it with four {{ hw('E') }} through the bracket.

### 3. Mount an SSD under the bracket

One 2.5-inch SSD mounts on the underside of the bracket, also with a 5.25-inch device fitted. Secure it with {{ hw('B') }} through the side of the bracket.

<p class="info-note" role="note">Mount these drives while the bracket is out of the chassis. Not every position can be used at the same time; check cable routing and cooling clearance for the chosen arrangement.</p>
