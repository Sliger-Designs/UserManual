A {{ hw('J') }} supports the GPU from the motherboard tray, preventing sag and shipping damage. Use the **tall brace** for a GPU without a backplate, or the **short brace** for a GPU with a backplate. Fit only one.

### 1. GPU without a backplate: free the mounting holes

Remove the two {{ hw('A') }} from the tray at the tall brace's position.

### 2. Fit the tall brace

Place the tall brace over the holes and secure it with the two {{ hw('K') }}.

### 3. GPU with a backplate: free the mounting holes

Remove the two {{ hw('A') }} from the tray at the short brace's position instead.

### 4. Fit the short brace

Place the short brace over the holes and secure it with the two {{ hw('K') }}.
