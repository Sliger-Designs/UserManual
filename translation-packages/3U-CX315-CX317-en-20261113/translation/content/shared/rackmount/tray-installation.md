### 1. Place and slide the tray

Lower the assembled tray into the chassis and slide it forward. Lift gently as needed to prevent its front edge catching on the chassis tabs.

### 2. Engage all six tabs

Continue forward until all six tabs are seated in the tray and its rear panel sits flush against the chassis rear.

### 3. Secure the tray

Install four {{ hw("E") }}, two on each side, through the chassis into the tray. Tighten by hand.
